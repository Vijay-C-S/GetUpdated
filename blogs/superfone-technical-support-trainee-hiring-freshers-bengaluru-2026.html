Superfone Hiring Technical Support Trainee in Bengaluru: Freshers Can Apply (2026)

November 24, 2026 by GetUpdated Team

Superfone Technical Support Trainee 2026

Superfone, a Bengaluru-based SaaS startup, is hiring a Technical Support Trainee for its HSR Layout office. The role is open to freshers and candidates with 0 to 1 years of experience, and it is a good entry point if you like technology and enjoy talking to customers.

The trainee period lasts 3 months and pays Rs 20k fixed plus Rs 5k incentive. High performers are considered for full-time conversion. The job was posted by the employer on LinkedIn about a day before this article, and applications run through LinkedIn Easy Apply.

This is not a typical voice-only support job. You will set up CRM tools, WhatsApp API and AI agents for new customers, using webhooks and APIs, so some technical comfort is expected. Read the full breakdown below before you apply.

About Superfone

Superfone describes itself as India's first AI-ready business phone app, built to help small and medium businesses manage customer interactions and grow. It is a fully app-based platform that combines telephony, WhatsApp, AI agents and other business software in one place.

The company says it is backed by top investors and industry experts, and that its team has experience from companies like Flipkart, PhonePe and Ola. For a fresher, that means a product-focused startup environment where you learn how a SaaS business works from the customer side.

Before your interview, the company asks candidates to read about Superfone on its website. Spend some time on it and understand who its customers are.

Role Overview

The Technical Support Trainee is mainly a customer-facing role. Right after a customer pays, you contact them, understand their use case and workflow, and help them set up Superfone's CRM, WhatsApp API and other capabilities.

Key responsibilities listed in the posting:

  • Understand the product's capabilities, integration possibilities and limitations.
  • Contact customers immediately after payment and understand their workflows.
  • Map customer requirements to product capabilities and guide them.
  • Integrate and troubleshoot CRM tools, WhatsApp API and AI agents.
  • Use webhooks, APIs, integrations and Superfone tools to complete customer setup.
  • Handle customer inquiries and resolve issues promptly.
  • Troubleshoot technical problems and find root causes.
  • Escalate unresolved issues to the right internal teams.
  • Record customer information and conversations in the CRM.
  • Show strong customer service, communication and ownership.

The company describes the ideal candidate as a competitive self-starter who understands tools and enjoys talking with customers.

Eligibility Criteria

The posting does not name a specific degree or branch. It sets these conditions instead:

  • Freshers or 0-1 years of experience are eligible.
  • Understanding of basic technology concepts: how APIs work, request/response structures and webhooks.
  • Ability to write basic scripting.
  • Fluent English and Hindi, spoken.
  • Excellent communication over video meetings, and follow-up over email and WhatsApp.
  • Good with Excel, Docs, MS Office or similar basic software.
  • An Android smartphone. The company does not provide one, so arrange one before joining.

Preferred but not mandatory:

  • Experience with Google Apps Script or similar platforms.
  • Simple projects or automations built with AI tools.
  • Exposure to Pabbly, Postman or Meta Platforms.
  • Prior experience, internship, part-time work or college experience.
  • Immediate joining.

Salary and Benefits

During the 3-month trainee period, Superfone offers Rs 20k fixed plus Rs 5k incentive. The incentive details are not given in the posting, so ask about the structure at the interview.

After conversion, the company mentions an "attractive package" without a figure, plus free medical and accident insurance. You also get structured on-the-job training in operations and customer handling, and you learn how SaaS products work.

Full-time conversion

Conversion is not automatic. The posting says high-performing trainees will be considered for full-time employment, so treat the 3 months as an extended evaluation.

Work Schedule and Location

The role is 100% on-site at the HSR Layout office in Bengaluru. The company works 6 days a week: five days in the office and work from home on Saturdays. If you live outside Bengaluru, plan your accommodation and commute before applying, since remote work is not an option on weekdays.

Selection Process

The posting does not describe the selection stages. For a role like this, expect a conversation about your communication and your technical basics, but that is a guess, not something the company has confirmed. Prepare for the following.

  • Introducing yourself clearly in both English and Hindi.
  • Explaining simple technical ideas, such as what an API or webhook does, in plain language.
  • Handling a mock customer scenario.
  • Showing any small project, script or automation you have built.

Preparation Plan

You do not need deep engineering knowledge, but you should be comfortable with the basics. A week of focused preparation can make a real difference.

  • APIs and webhooks: Learn what a request and response look like, what HTTP methods and status codes mean, and how a webhook differs from an API call.
  • Postman: Practise sending simple requests to a public API and reading the JSON response.
  • Basic scripting: Try a small Google Apps Script or Python script that automates a spreadsheet task.
  • WhatsApp Business API: Read about what it is and how businesses use it with CRMs.
  • Communication: Practise explaining a technical idea to a non-technical person, aloud, in both English and Hindi.
  • Excel and Docs: Brush up on formulas, filters and clear documentation.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Applying without reading about Superfone. The company explicitly asks you to.
  • Claiming API knowledge you cannot explain. Be honest and show what you have actually tried.
  • Ignoring the Android phone requirement and the on-site, 6-day schedule.
  • Sending a resume with no mention of projects, tools or automations.
  • Weak spoken Hindi or English. Both are mandatory.

Check the details

The posting asks for an Android device and on-site work in HSR Layout. Make sure both work for you before applying.
