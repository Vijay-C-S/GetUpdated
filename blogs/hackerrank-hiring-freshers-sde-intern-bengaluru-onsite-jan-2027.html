HackerRank Hiring SDE Intern 2027: Paid 6-Month Onsite Internship in Bengaluru

November 20, 2026 by GetUpdated Team

HackerRank is hiring SDE Interns for Jan-June 2027

HackerRank is hiring Software Development Engineer (SDE) Interns for a 6-month, full-time, onsite internship at its Bengaluru office, running from January to June 2027. The posting is on the company's official Greenhouse careers board, and the internship is paid.

The role suits final-year students and recent graduates who enjoy writing code and want real production exposure. According to the posting, interns are paired with a mentor and get to push code to production within the first month. This guide covers what the official page says, what it leaves out, and how to prepare.

Because the internship is strictly in-office for the full duration, read the availability condition carefully before you apply.

About HackerRank

HackerRank helps companies such as NVIDIA, Amazon and Microsoft hire and upskill developers based on skills rather than pedigree. Its platform is trusted by over 2,500 companies to build engineering teams. Most Indian students know it from coding practice and contests, but the company's core business is skills-based technical hiring.

The company says software is entering an era where humans and AI build side by side, and that the definition of strong technical talent is changing. Its stated working principles include caring about the craft, working as one team, putting developers first, going deep, and owning outcomes. Expect a culture that values high standards and urgency.

Role Overview

HackerRank is looking for high-potential "Hacker Interns" to join its full-time engineers as it builds an engaged developer community. The posting stresses writing great code that other engineers around the world will use. The page does not list a detailed tech stack or day-to-day task list, so treat the specific technologies as something to confirm during the interview process.

What the posting does promise:

  • A mentor assigned to each intern for the whole internship.
  • Pushing code to production and contributing to real-world projects from the first month.
  • Weekly access to company progress updates directly from the Executive team.
  • Direct access to all employees, and the freedom to challenge the status quo.
  • Consideration for a returning offer, either a full-time role after graduation or another internship the next year.

Every intern batch also designs its own page, and past batches' pages and blogs are linked from the posting. They are worth reading before you apply.

Eligibility

The official excerpt does not state a degree, branch, CGPA cut-off or graduation year. The one clear condition is availability. Check the posting itself for any updates before applying.

  • You must be available full-time for the entire 6 months (January to June 2027).
  • You must be able to work from the Bengaluru office throughout. Remote work is not an option.
  • A genuine interest in writing quality code and learning from mentors.
  • Degree, branch and year: not disclosed in the page text we read.

Check your college calendar

The posting says to apply only if you can work full-time for the whole duration. If your college requires you to attend semester classes, exams or a project review in Jan to June 2027, sort that out with your institution first.

Stipend and Benefits

The FAQ confirms this is a paid internship, but the stipend amount is not disclosed on the official page. Do not trust unofficial figures on social media. If you reach the offer stage, ask the recruiter for the exact number in writing.

Beyond the stipend, the posting lists insurance, coverage of two-way travel, and various reimbursements. Since you need to relocate to or stay in Bengaluru, the travel coverage helps students coming from other cities. Ask about accommodation separately, as the page says nothing about it.

Selection Process

The posting does not describe the selection rounds. As a developer-assessment company, HackerRank is likely to test coding ability, but that is an inference and not an official statement. A sensible plan is to prepare for an online coding assessment and one or more technical conversations, and to be ready for questions about your projects.

The company also notes that its recruiters use @hackerrank.com email addresses and never ask for payment or credit-check information to apply, interview or work there. If anyone asks you for money, treat it as a scam.

How to Apply

  1. Open the official Greenhouse posting using the link in the Official Links section below.
  2. Read the full description again and confirm you are free for January to June 2027.
  3. Prepare your resume. There is no mandatory format, but HackerRank suggests building one at hackerrank.com/resume.
  4. Fill in the application form with accurate details and submit it.
  5. For questions about the process, write to internship-queries@hackerrank.com.

Apply early

No closing date is shown, so the posting could close whenever the team has enough applicants. With the batch starting in January 2027, do not wait.

Preparation Plan

Start with fundamentals. Revise arrays, strings, hash maps, linked lists, trees, graphs, recursion and basic dynamic programming, and practise on timed problems. Since HackerRank is a coding platform, getting comfortable solving problems in an online editor will serve you well.

Next, build or polish two or three projects you can explain end to end. Be ready to describe why you made each design choice, what broke and how you fixed it. Put code on GitHub with a clear README.

  • Weeks 1 to 2: data structures and easy/medium problems daily.
  • Weeks 3 to 4: one core language in depth, plus basics of databases, REST APIs and Git.
  • Weeks 5 to 6: mock interviews, project walkthroughs, and reading past intern blogs.

Read the intern pages

The posting links to past batches' pages and blogs by interns. They show what projects interns actually worked on and what the culture is like, which gives you good material for your interview answers.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Applying without being able to commit to the full 6 months onsite.
  • Sending a long, unfocused resume. Keep it to one page with projects and skills first.
  • Memorising solutions instead of understanding patterns.
  • Trusting third-party sites that claim a stipend figure or a deadline. Verify on the official page.
  • Ignoring the company's values. Showing ownership and curiosity matters as much as code.

  • I can work from the Bengaluru office.
  • My resume is ready and my projects are on GitHub.
  • I have practised timed coding problems.
  • I will only trust @hackerrank.com emails and will never pay anyone.

Final Takeaway

This is a strong opportunity for freshers who want hands-on engineering experience: mentorship, production code from month one, direct access to leadership and a possible returning offer. The trade-offs are the strict onsite, full-time commitment and the undisclosed stipend and eligibility details.

If you fit the availability requirement, prepare your coding fundamentals and apply through the official link in the Official Links section above, soon.
