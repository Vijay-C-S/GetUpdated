HCLTech Graduate Trainee Hiring 2026: Global Service Desk, Pan India, Any Graduate (2024/2025/2026 Batch)

HCLTech Graduate Trainee - Global Service Desk 2026

HCLTech has opened a Candidate Registration Form for Graduate Trainees across India. The role named on the form is Global Service Desk, and the form is open to any fresher graduate from the 2024, 2025 and 2026 batches who meets the percentage criteria.

This is a good chance for non-IT graduates (BA, BCom, BSc, BBA and others) as well as engineers, because the degree requirement is simply "Any Fresher Graduate". The catch is a 12-month service agreement with a payment liability if you leave early, so read the terms before you submit.

The form does not state a last date, a vacancy count or an exact salary. Treat it as open now and register early.

About HCLTech

HCLTech is a global technology company headquartered in India, working in IT services, engineering and digital operations for clients worldwide. It hires large batches of fresher graduates every year for support, infrastructure and business process roles.

Service desk teams are the first point of contact for customers' technology problems. For many freshers, this is a practical entry into the IT services industry, with scope to move into infrastructure, cloud, security or other support streams later.

Role Overview: Global Service Desk

The form names the role as Global Service Desk. The detailed job description is not given on the form, so the points below are general for service desk work and not HCLTech-specific facts.

  • Handling user queries and incidents over calls, chat or email.
  • Logging tickets and following them until resolution.
  • Basic troubleshooting of accounts, passwords, software and devices.
  • Communicating clearly with international users, which may involve shift work.

Shift timings and work mode are not stated, so confirm them during the selection process.

Eligibility Criteria

As given on the registration form:

  • UG degree: Any fresher graduate.
  • Passing year: 2024, 2025 or 2026.
  • Marks: 60% and above, as per the percentage eligibility criteria.
  • Location preference: Pan India.
  • Acceptance of the 12-month service agreement.

The form also asks for 10th, 12th/Diploma and UG percentages, so keep your marksheets ready. It has fields for PG degree/course, which suggests PG candidates may also fill it, but the stated requirement is an UG degree.

Check your marks first

The form says CTC is based on the 60% and above criteria. If your aggregate is below 60%, your application is unlikely to move forward. Do not submit wrong percentages, because the form asks you to confirm all details are correct.

Salary and Service Agreement

The form says CTC is offered as per the standard HCLTech grid. No figure is disclosed, so do not rely on unofficial numbers shared on social media.

The service agreement is the most important term:

  • The agreement is for 12 months (as applicable).
  • If you leave before completing 12 months, you are liable to pay 50K to the organisation.

Read the bond clause

Only register if you are ready to stay for at least a year. Ask HR for the written terms before you sign anything.

Selection Process

The form does not describe the selection rounds. Registration is the first step, and shortlisted candidates are likely to be contacted by HCLTech. Based on general fresher hiring for service desk roles, expect some combination of an online assessment, a communication check and an HR interview. This is an expectation, not a confirmed process.

How to Apply

  1. Open the registration form from the Official Links section below.
  2. Read the instructions at the top carefully.
  3. Enter your full name, email ID, contact number and an alternate number.
  4. Fill in your permanent address, city (as on Aadhaar), state and pin code.
  5. Enter 10th, 12th/Diploma and UG percentages, UG course, college name and passing year.
  6. Select your Location Applied For and fill in your HCLTech Application Number, if you have one.
  7. Tick the declaration that you have read the eligibility criteria and that all details are correct.
  8. Submit the form and save a screenshot or confirmation.

Preparation Plan

Even though the process is not described, you can prepare in a simple way.

  • Communication: Practise speaking clearly in English. Record yourself introducing your education and strengths.
  • Basics of IT support: Learn what a ticket, incident, SLA and ITIL are, and how to reset passwords or troubleshoot common problems.
  • Aptitude: Revise percentages, ratios, series and basic reasoning.
  • Typing and email writing: Practise short, professional emails.
  • Documents: Keep marksheets, ID proof and your Aadhaar address ready.

Common Interview Topics

  • Tell me about yourself and why you want a service desk role.
  • Are you comfortable with shifts and customer-facing work?
  • How would you handle an angry customer?
  • Basic computer, network and software questions.
  • Your understanding of the 12-month service agreement.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Entering a wrong passing year or percentage.
  • Using an email or phone number that you do not check.
  • Ignoring the service agreement clause.
  • Using an address that does not match your Aadhaar, since the form asks for the city as on Aadhaar.
  • Waiting too long, since no last date is stated and the form may close without notice.

Final Takeaway

Any 2024/2025/2026 graduate with 60%+ can register for HCLTech's Graduate Trainee (Global Service Desk) role. Read the 12-month service agreement before you apply.

This is an open door for freshers from any stream, but it comes with a bond. If you are ready for customer support work and a one-year commitment, fill in the form carefully and register early.
