NLC India Graduate Executive Trainee Recruitment 2026: 143 Posts via GATE 2025

November 23, 2026 by GetUpdated Team

NLC India GET recruitment 2026 through GATE 2025

NLC India Limited (NLCIL), a Navratna Government of India enterprise, has issued Advt. No. 10/2026 for 143 Graduate Executive Trainee (GET) posts in Mechanical, Electrical, Civil and Mining engineering. Selection is based on your GATE 2025 score, so no separate written exam is mentioned in the portion of the notice we could read.

The role is fresher-friendly: no work experience is required, the upper age limit is 30 years for the general category (as on 01-11-2026), and the trainee pay starts at a basic of INR 50,000 per month in the E-2 grade. If you wrote GATE 2025 in ME, EE, CE or MN and scored well, read this guide fully before applying.

Check the last date first

The part of the notice we could read does not show the registration window or last date. Open the official notification and the NLCIL careers portal today and confirm the closing date before you start. Do not wait for the final days.

About NLC India Limited

NLC India Limited is a Navratna public sector enterprise. As per the notice, it reported a consolidated turnover of about INR 17,490 crore for FY 2025-26. Its work spans lignite and coal mining, thermal power generation and renewable energy, and it is expanding capacity under an ambitious corporate plan.

The company operates in Neyveli (Tamil Nadu), Barsingsar (Rajasthan), Talabira (Odisha), South Pachwara (Jharkhand), and solar and wind sites across several states. Its subsidiaries and joint ventures, NTPL in Tuticorin and NUPPL in Ghatampur (Uttar Pradesh), are also covered by this recruitment. For an engineer, this means a PSU career with a mix of plant, mine and project exposure.

Vacancy Details

The 143 posts are split by discipline as below. Posts are also divided into four areas: Area 1 (thermal power stations), Area 2 (mines and allied services), Area 3 (renewable energy, projects and planning) and Area 4 (services and support). You apply for an area, and the posting follows your choice and company needs.

Eligibility Criteria

  • GATE: You must have qualified GATE 2025 in the relevant code (ME, EE, CE or MN). Only GATE 2025 marks are valid for this recruitment.
  • Mechanical: Full/part-time Bachelor degree in Mechanical Engineering or Mechanical and Production Engineering.
  • Electrical: Electrical Engineering, Electrical and Electronics Engineering or Power Engineering.
  • Civil: Civil Engineering or Civil and Structural Engineering.
  • Mining: Bachelor degree in Mining Engineering.
  • Marks: Minimum 60% for UR/EWS/OBC (NCL) and 50% for SC/ST, calculated on aggregate of all years or semesters.
  • Degree source: From an Indian university or institute recognised by UGC, AICTE or another statutory authority.

No rounding of percentage

The notice says rounding is not acceptable under any circumstances. A 59.6% will not be treated as 60%. If your university gives only CGPA, convert it to the exact equivalent percentage as per the notice (for example, 6.0 on a 10-point scale is 60%). Equivalent or combined branches outside the notified list are not considered.

You must hold the qualification as on the crucial date, 01-11-2026. Final-year students who have not yet passed are not eligible.

Age Limit and Relaxation

CategoryUpper age (as on 01-11-2026)
UR / EWS30 years
OBC (NCL)33 years

Relaxation is 5 years for SC/ST, 3 years for OBC (NCL) and 10 years for persons with benchmark disabilities (13 years for PwBD-OBC and 15 years for PwBD-SC/ST). Ex-servicemen get relaxation as per Government of India guidelines. In every case, the maximum age is 58 years on the crucial date. SC/ST/OBC (NCL)/EWS candidates applying against unreserved posts must meet the UR norms.

Salary, Training and Benefits

GETs undergo one year of training. During this period you receive a basic pay of INR 50,000 per month plus applicable Dearness Allowance and a Common Allowance of 35% of basic pay. The grade is E-2 with a scale of INR 50,000 to 1,60,000, and the approximate CTC is INR 14.50 lakh per annum.

  • Annual Performance Related Pay, medical treatment for self and dependents, and group insurance as per rules.
  • Unfurnished residential accommodation at standard rent in company townships, subject to availability.
  • After successful training, absorption in E-3 grade with scale INR 60,000 to 1,80,000 and basic pay of INR 60,000 per month.
  • Superannuation benefit of 30% of basic pay plus DA.

Selected trainees cannot seek transfer to another plant or unit for the first 3 years from joining.

Place of Posting

You will be posted for training and work anywhere in the Area you applied for, depending on organisational needs. That can mean Neyveli, Rajasthan, Odisha, Jharkhand, or renewable project sites in various states. Choose your area honestly, since remote mine sites and solar or wind projects have a very different lifestyle from a township plant posting.

Because the three-year no-transfer rule applies, think about location before you apply and not after you receive an offer.

Application Fee and Selection Process

The part of the notice we could read says SC/ST/PwBD/Ex-servicemen candidates are exempt from the application fee, but must pay a non-refundable processing fee of INR 354 (including 18% GST). The fee for other categories and the full selection stages (GATE score weightage, interview, document checks) were not visible in the readable text, so confirm them in the official notification.

The recruitment is based on GATE 2025 marks, so your GATE score is the main factor in shortlisting. Keep your GATE scorecard and registration details ready.

How to Apply

  1. Download and read Advt. No. 10/2026 completely from the official link in the Official Links section below.
  2. Confirm your branch, GATE 2025 code, aggregate percentage and age on 01-11-2026.
  3. Open the NLCIL online recruitment portal through the official link below and register with a valid email and mobile number.
  4. Fill in GATE 2025 details (registration number, score, code), education marks and category details.
  5. Upload documents as asked and pay the applicable fee online.
  6. Submit the form, then save the final PDF and payment receipt for later stages.

Keep documents ready

Keep your degree marksheets, GATE scorecard, category certificate (OBC-NCL must be a valid non-creamy layer certificate, EWS and disability certificates if relevant) and photo scan ready before you open the form.

Preparation Plan for Freshers

Since your GATE score is already fixed, your preparation now is about documents and interview readiness. Revise core subjects of your branch: for Mechanical, thermodynamics, heat transfer, boilers, turbines and fluid machines; for Electrical, power systems, machines, protection and switchgear; for Civil, structures, concrete and construction management; for Mining, mine ventilation, surveying and mining methods.

Also read about NLCIL's lignite mines, thermal units and renewable projects. A PSU interview commonly checks whether you understand the industry you are joining, so know basics such as how a lignite-fired power plant works and what solar and wind plants involve.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Applying with a branch not on the notified list, or with 59.x% marks.
  • Using a GATE score from a year other than 2025.
  • Submitting an OBC certificate that is not non-creamy layer or is outdated.
  • Ignoring the crucial date of 01-11-2026 for age and qualification.
  • Waiting for the last day, when the portal can be slow.

FAQs

1. How many posts are in NLC India GET recruitment 2026?

Advt. No. 10/2026 has 143 posts: Mechanical 66, Electrical 44, Civil 23 and Mining 10.

2. Is GATE 2025 score required?

Yes. Only GATE 2025 marks in the relevant code (ME, EE, CE or MN) are valid for this recruitment.

3. What is the age limit?

30 years for UR/EWS, 33 for OBC (NCL) and 35 for SC/ST as on 01-11-2026, with further relaxation for PwBD and ex-servicemen.

4. What is the minimum percentage required?

60% for UR/EWS/OBC (NCL) and 50% for SC/ST in the notified degree. Rounding off is not accepted.

5. What is the salary of a Graduate Executive Trainee?

Basic pay is INR 50,000 per month plus DA and allowances during one-year training, with a CTC of about INR 14.50 lakh per annum. After training, basic becomes INR 60,000 in E-3 grade.

6. Is work experience needed?

No. The notice does not require any work experience.

7. Is there a fee exemption?

SC/ST/PwBD/Ex-servicemen are exempt from the application fee but pay a non-refundable INR 354 processing fee including GST.

8. Where will I be posted?

Anywhere in the Area you applied for, such as Neyveli, Rajasthan, Odisha, Jharkhand or renewable project sites. Transfers are not allowed for the first 3 years.
