ISRO SAC Graduate, Technician and Trade Apprentice Recruitment 2026: Apply Online by 2 December

November 23, 2026 by GetUpdated Team

ISRO SAC Ahmedabad apprentice recruitment 2026

ISRO's Space Applications Centre (SAC), Ahmedabad has opened applications for Graduate, Technician and Trade Apprentices under Advertisement No. SAC: 03:2026 dated 11-11-2026. The one-year apprenticeship is registered under the Apprentices Act, 1961, and covers 18 post codes across engineering, commerce, computer applications, arts, diploma and ITI trades.

Online registration closes at 1700 hours on 02-12-2026. With only days left from today (23 November 2026), freshers who qualify should prepare their documents and apply now.

One condition decides who can apply: Graduate and Technician Apprentices must hold a degree or diploma from a recognised institution in Maharashtra, Gujarat, Goa, Chhattisgarh, Madhya Pradesh, Rajasthan or Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu. Trade Apprentices need an ITI from a recognised institute in Gujarat. Read the eligibility section carefully before you start.

About ISRO Space Applications Centre

SAC, Ahmedabad is one of the lead centres of the Indian Space Research Organisation. According to the notice, it designs space-borne instruments for ISRO's terrestrial and planetary missions. It also develops and operationalises space technology for national development.

For a fresher, an apprenticeship here means a year inside a working space-technology lab. You see how payloads, electronics, software, mechanical systems and facilities are handled at a national centre. That line on a CV helps for later private and government applications, though the notice does not promise a job after training.

Eligibility Checklist

  • Indian national only.
  • Age on 02-12-2026: 18 to 28 years for post codes 01 to 09 (Graduate), and 18 to 35 years for post codes 10 to 26 (Technician and Trade).
  • Upper age relaxation: 3 years for OBC (NCL), 5 years for SC/ST. PwBD relaxation follows Government of India rules.
  • Degree/diploma from a recognised university/board/institute in Maharashtra, Gujarat, Goa, Chhattisgarh, Madhya Pradesh, Rajasthan or DNH & DD. ITI from a recognised institute in Gujarat for Trade posts.
  • Not currently undergoing, or already having undergone, apprenticeship training.
  • No job experience of one year or more after getting the required qualification, as on the date of joining as an apprentice.

Check your institution's state

Eligibility depends on where your degree, diploma or ITI was awarded, not where you live. If your college is outside the listed states, the notice does not make you eligible for these posts.

Stipend

The notice gives these monthly stipends, subject to revisions as per orders from time to time:

  • Graduate Apprentice: ₹12,300 per month
  • Technician Apprentice: ₹10,900 per month
  • Trade Apprentice: ₹10,560 per month for post codes 15 to 16, and ₹11,040 per month for post codes 17 to 26

The training period is one year. Apprentices are engaged, not hired as regular employees, so treat this as a training stage and not a permanent job.

Selection Process

The notice says candidates will be shortlisted on the percentage of marks in the qualifying exam (degree, diploma or ITI) declared by a recognised university/board/institute. You must enter your marks as a percentage and be ready to provide documentary proof of your claim.

The excerpt we read does not describe the later stages in detail. Read the remaining pages of the PDF for any document verification, interview or medical steps. Keep your mark sheets, certificates and category proofs ready in case you are called.

Your qualifying-exam percentage is the main shortlisting factor, so enter it accurately.

How to Apply

  1. Read the full detailed advertisement PDF, including the pages after the general conditions.
  2. Check that your qualification, state of institution and age on 02-12-2026 match your chosen post code.
  3. Open the SAC careers portal through the official link in the Official Links section below.
  4. Register and fill in your personal and academic details, with your qualifying-exam percentage.
  5. Upload the documents requested on the form.
  6. Review everything, submit before 1700 hrs on 02-12-2026, and save the confirmation.

Preparation Tips

Because shortlisting is based on marks, your effort now goes into a clean application. Scan your final-year mark sheet, degree or diploma certificate and ID in clear, readable files. Make sure your name and date of birth match across documents.

If you are called for an interview or verification, revise your core subjects: electronics fundamentals, basic programming and data structures for E&C and CS/IT candidates, or your trade basics for ITI candidates. Also be ready to explain your final-year project in simple words.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Applying without checking that your institution is in the listed states.
  • Choosing the wrong post code for your branch.
  • Entering CGPA instead of the aggregate percentage.
  • Ignoring the one-year experience and prior-apprenticeship exclusions.
  • Waiting for the final day to register.

Keep proof ready

The notice says you must support your claimed percentage with documents. Keep a digital copy of every mark sheet in one folder so you can upload quickly.

Final Takeaway

This is a useful one-year opportunity at one of ISRO's lead centres for graduates, diploma holders and ITI candidates from the eligible regions. The deadline is close, so check your eligibility, gather your documents and apply through the official link in the Official Links section above.

If you are outside the eligible states, do not apply, but keep watching official notices for other apprenticeship drives that suit you.

FAQs

1. What is the last date to apply for ISRO SAC apprentice 2026?

Online registration closes at 1700 hours on 02-12-2026.

2. Who can apply for Graduate Apprentice posts?

Candidates with B.E./B.Tech. with minimum 60% (or graduation with 60% for B.Com, BCA, BA) aged 18 to 28 years as on 02-12-2026.

3. What is the stipend?

₹12,300 for Graduate, ₹10,900 for Technician, and ₹10,560 or ₹11,040 for Trade Apprentices per month, depending on the post code.

4. Can candidates from Karnataka apply?

Only if the degree or diploma is from a recognised institution in Maharashtra, Gujarat, Goa, Chhattisgarh, Madhya Pradesh, Rajasthan or DNH & DD. Trade posts need an ITI from Gujarat.

5. What is the age limit for Technician and Trade posts?

18 to 35 years as on 02-12-2026, with 3 years relaxation for OBC (NCL) and 5 years for SC/ST.

6. How long is the training?

The apprenticeship lasts one year and is registered under the Apprentices Act, 1961.

7. How are candidates shortlisted?

Shortlisting is based on the percentage obtained in the qualifying degree, diploma or ITI exam, supported by documents.

8. Who is not eligible?

Those who have done or are doing apprenticeship, or have one year or more of job experience after the required qualification as on the joining date.
