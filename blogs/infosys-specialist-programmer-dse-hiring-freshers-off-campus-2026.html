Infosys Specialist Programmer / Digital Specialist Engineer 2026

Infosys has opened an off-campus registration form for Specialist Programmer (SP) and Digital Specialist Engineer (DSE) roles. It is hosted on the company's own survey portal. These two tracks are among the best-known entry routes into Infosys for freshers who are strong at coding.

The form page loads only in a modern browser and shows no details to a plain fetch. So the eligibility and pay below come from a job-site article on the earlier SP/DSE drive, and we mark those points as reported. The batch, deadline and exact criteria for this December form are not disclosed in the text we could read. Check them on the form before you apply.

If you are a CSE, IT, ECE or EEE graduate with good problem-solving skills, read this guide first. Then keep your resume and a few strong projects ready before you open the form.

About Infosys

Infosys is one of India's largest IT services companies, headquartered in Bengaluru. It hires thousands of freshers every year through campus drives, the InfyTQ and HackWithInfy programmes, and off-campus registrations like this one. Its main training campus is in Mysuru, and many freshers start their careers there.

For a fresher, the draw is structured training, work on global client projects, and a clear path to grow through levels. The SP track is meant for strong coders and is paid higher than the standard Systems Engineer role.

Role Overview: SP and DSE

Specialist Programmer (SP) is a high-skill coding role. The job site reports three levels, L1, L2 and L3, and your level depends on how you perform in the assessment. The SP role was earlier called Power Programmer. Expect work on complex engineering problems, algorithms and building products.

Digital Specialist Engineer (DSE) is a trainee role for graduates with good programming ability and digital skills. It sits above the standard entry role in both expectations and pay. You would be trained first, then placed on a project.

Both roles need solid fundamentals in data structures, algorithms and at least one language such as Java, Python or C++.

Eligibility Criteria

The points below are as reported by a job site. Confirm them on the official form.

  • Degree: B.E/B.Tech, M.E/M.Tech, MCA or 5-year integrated M.Sc.
  • Branches: CSE, IT, ISE, AI/ML, Data Science, Cybersecurity, ECE, EEE and other CSE-allied streams.
  • Marks: Generally 60% or 6.5 CGPA and above throughout.
  • Backlogs: No active backlogs.
  • Batch: The earlier article listed 2024 and 2025 graduates. The batch for the December form is not disclosed, so read the form's eligibility line.

Check the batch first

Passout batch rules change between Infosys forms. If your graduation year is not accepted on the form, do not apply through a different route and expect it to work.

Selection Process

The reported process has three stages.

  1. Online assessment: Focused on data structures, algorithms and competitive programming.
  2. Technical interview: Covers your projects, live coding and CS fundamentals.
  3. HR interview: Checks communication, relocation readiness and your reasons for joining Infosys.

The SP level you get (L1, L2 or L3) is said to depend on your performance in the InfyTQ or HackWithInfy score, or in the off-campus assessment. So a strong coding round can raise both your chance of selection and your pay band.

How to Apply

  1. Open the registration form from the Official Links section below, using Chrome, Edge or Firefox. Internet Explorer is not supported.
  2. If the page says your browser version is old, refresh it.
  3. Fill in your personal, education and contact details exactly as they appear in your certificates.
  4. Select the role track (SP or DSE) if the form asks.
  5. Upload an updated resume that shows your programming languages, projects and internships.
  6. Submit the form and save the confirmation email or screenshot.
  7. Watch your email and spam folder for the assessment invite.

Registration is free according to the sources we read. Infosys does not ask candidates for money to get a job.

Preparation Plan

Start with the assessment, because it filters most candidates.

  • Data structures: Arrays, strings, linked lists, stacks, queues, trees, graphs, heaps and hash maps.
  • Algorithms: Sorting, binary search, recursion, dynamic programming, greedy methods and graph traversal.
  • Practice: Solve timed problems daily on LeetCode, HackerRank or CodeChef. Aim for easy and medium problems to start with, then add harder ones if you target SP.
  • Language: Pick one of Java, Python or C++ and know its standard library well.
  • CS basics: DBMS and SQL queries, operating systems, computer networks and OOP concepts.

ECE and EEE students should not skip this. Many get through on coding practice alone. Add a short revision of your branch basics only if you are asked about them.

Interview Topics

Expect questions drawn from your own resume. Be ready to explain every project, the tech stack you used and what you personally built.

  • Live coding on a laptop or shared editor, with the reasoning explained aloud.
  • Time and space complexity of your solutions.
  • SQL joins, normalisation and indexing.
  • OOP pillars with simple examples.
  • HR questions such as strengths, weaknesses, relocation and working in shifts or teams.

Explain while you code

Interviewers score your approach as well as your answer. Talk through your logic, mention edge cases, then optimise.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Applying without reading the batch and marks criteria on the form.
  • Using a resume that lists skills you cannot discuss.
  • Leaving the registration to the last minute. The deadline is not disclosed, so apply early.
  • Trusting social media pages that ask for a fee or share non-Infosys links.
  • Entering a wrong email or phone number, so you miss the assessment invite.
  • Ignoring the service-agreement terms in the offer letter.

Apply early through the official Infosys form, and spend your time on coding practice, because the online assessment decides most outcomes.

Verify them on the form, prepare steadily, and avoid anyone who asks you to pay.
