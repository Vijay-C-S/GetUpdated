About CGI

CGI is a global IT and business consulting company. It works with organisations across industries on technology, consulting and digital transformation. Its work spans software development, cloud services, data and analytics, cybersecurity and emerging technologies.

CGI calls its employees "CGI Partners". The company promotes collaboration, ownership, continuous learning and professional development. For a fresher, a services and consulting firm like this means exposure to different client problems and technology stacks early in your career.

Role Overview: What a Graduate Trainee – AI & ML Does

CGI is looking for enthusiastic fresh graduates who want to build a career in AI, ML, Generative AI, Data Analytics and related technologies. This is a trainee position. You are expected to learn on the job while contributing to real project work, not to arrive as an expert.

Based on the listing, the responsibilities include:

  • Supporting the development, testing and implementation of AI-driven solutions and automation initiatives.
  • Working with business and technical teams to understand requirements and turn them into technology solutions.
  • Assisting in developing and deploying Machine Learning and Generative AI use cases.
  • Using AI platforms and tools to improve operational efficiency and business outcomes.
  • Analysing data, generating insights and contributing to data-driven decisions.
  • Following emerging technologies, industry trends and best practices in AI and Data Science.
  • Taking part in innovation programmes, hackathons, proof-of-concepts and experimentation.
  • Preparing presentations, reports and project updates for stakeholders.

Note that the role is not purely coding. Several points involve requirements gathering, reporting and stakeholder communication. Candidates who can explain technical work in simple language have an advantage.

Preparation Plan

A focused two-to-three week plan can get you ready.

Week 1: Python and data basics

  • Revise Python: data structures, functions, OOP basics, file handling and exception handling.
  • Practise with NumPy and Pandas: cleaning, grouping and merging data.
  • Revise SQL basics, since analytics roles often use it.

Week 2: ML and GenAI concepts

  • Revise supervised and unsupervised learning, overfitting, bias and variance, cross-validation and evaluation metrics (accuracy, precision, recall, F1).
  • Be able to explain regression, decision trees, random forests and clustering in simple words.
  • Learn what Generative AI, large language models, prompts and embeddings are, and where they fit in business use cases.

Week 3: Cloud, projects and communication

  • Get an overview of Azure, AWS or Google Cloud services for AI and storage.
  • Prepare a two-minute explanation of each project: problem, data, method, result and what you would improve.
  • Practise a short self-introduction and explaining a technical idea to a non-technical person.

Likely Interview Topics

These topics follow the skills in the listing. They are preparation suggestions, not a confirmed question list.

  • Difference between AI, ML, deep learning and Generative AI.
  • How you would handle missing data or an imbalanced dataset.
  • Choosing a metric for a classification problem.
  • What prompts are and how to reduce wrong answers from an LLM.
  • Use cases for AI tools such as Copilot or ChatGPT in a business process.
  • Basic Python coding: lists, dictionaries, string handling.
  • Your project: why you chose that approach and what failed.
  • Teamwork and learning examples, such as a hackathon or group project.
