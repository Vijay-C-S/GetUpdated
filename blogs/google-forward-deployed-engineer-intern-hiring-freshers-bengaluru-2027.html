Google Forward Deployed Engineer Intern 2027 Hiring in Bengaluru: Eligibility and How to Apply

November 19, 2026 by GetUpdated Team

Google Forward Deployed Engineer Intern 2027, Bengaluru

Google has opened applications for the Forward Deployed Engineer Intern, 2027 role in Bengaluru, Karnataka, inside its Google Cloud Consulting team. The role is listed on Google's official careers site under the "Intern & Apprentice" category, and it is aimed at students who are in the penultimate year of a Bachelor's or Master's degree.

This is not a typical coding internship. You would work as an embedded builder with customers, turning frontier AI products into production-grade solutions. If you have already built RAG pipelines, agents or cloud-hosted AI projects, this is a role worth a serious application.

Below is a verified breakdown of the eligibility, responsibilities, skills, a preparation plan and the way to apply. Everything factual comes from the official Google Careers posting.

About Google Cloud Consulting

Google Cloud helps organisations digitally transform their business using enterprise-grade solutions built on Google's technology. According to the posting, customers in more than 200 countries and territories rely on Google Cloud to solve critical business problems.

The Consulting team works hand-in-hand with those customers. Interns on this team are expected to be close to real deployments, not just internal prototypes, which makes it an unusually practical learning environment for a student.

Role Overview: What a Forward Deployed Engineer Does

Google describes the role as an embedded builder who bridges the gap between frontier AI products and production-grade reality. Instead of staying at high-level architecture, you code, debug and jointly ship bespoke agentic solutions inside customer environments.

You would also help solve integration, data readiness and state-management challenges, and act as a feedback loop that shapes the Google Cloud product roadmap. Google says it wants early-career, high-agency engineers with a founder's mindset.

Think of it as software engineering plus customer problem-solving, centred on AI agents.

Key Responsibilities

  • Scale rapid proofs-of-concept into robust, production-grade multi-agent workflows and cloud solutions.
  • Write efficient, high-quality code and maintain rigorous standards in code reviews.
  • Build secure APIs and data pipelines connecting Google AI and cloud technology to enterprise infrastructure.
  • Develop high-performance evaluation and debugging frameworks for accuracy, safety and low latency.
  • Partner with client engineering teams to promote Google-grade development practices and long-term adoption.
  • Turn field insights and roadblocks into formal feature requests and reusable core modules.
  • Drive full-stack project priorities and infrastructure challenges end-to-end with accountability.

Notice how much of this is about reliability: evaluation, debugging, security and latency. A demo notebook is not enough; Google wants people who care whether the system keeps working.

Eligibility Criteria

Minimum qualifications listed by Google:

  • Currently pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Engineering or a related technical field.
  • Enrolled in the penultimate academic year of the Bachelor's or Master's degree.
  • Experience in Python, and in data structures and algorithms.
  • Experience taking production-grade AI-driven solutions from conception to launch, and architecting AI systems on cloud platforms such as Google Cloud Platform.
  • Experience building pipelines for structured and unstructured data using vector databases and RAG-like architectures for enterprise AI.

Preferred qualifications:

  • Implementing multi-agent systems with frameworks such as LangGraph, CrewAI or ADK, and patterns such as ReAct, self-reflection and hierarchical delegation.
  • Experience leading technical discovery sessions.
  • Knowledge of LLM-native metrics (tokens/sec, cost-per-request) and techniques for state management and granular tracing.

Check your year

The role is for the penultimate year only. Final-year students and graduates may not fit the stated criteria, so confirm your academic year before applying.

Salary and Stipend

The official posting excerpt does not disclose the stipend. We are therefore not quoting any figure. Details, if any, are typically shared by the recruiter during the process, so ask politely at the right stage.

Selection Process

The posting does not describe the hiring stages, so we will not invent them. Google's intern hiring generally involves an application review and technical conversations, but treat that as general context, not a confirmed schedule for this role.

Given the listed skills, expect questions on data structures and algorithms, Python, and how you have built and shipped AI systems. Be ready to discuss your projects in depth, including trade-offs, failures and what you measured.

How to Apply

  1. Open the official Google Careers listing through the link in the Official Links section below.
  2. Sign in with your Google account and complete your Careers profile.
  3. Upload an up-to-date resume that highlights Python, AI and cloud projects.
  4. Mention your degree, branch and the year you are currently in, so that penultimate-year status is clear.
  5. Add links to your GitHub, deployed demos or write-ups.
  6. Review everything and submit. Save the confirmation email.

Apply early

No closing date is shown, so do not wait. Roles at big companies can be filled or closed without much notice.

Preparation Plan

Weeks 1-2: fundamentals. Revise arrays, hash maps, trees, graphs, recursion and dynamic programming. Solve problems daily in Python and practise explaining your approach aloud.

Weeks 3-4: RAG project. Build an end-to-end RAG app: ingest documents, chunk, embed, store in a vector database, retrieve and answer. Add an evaluation script that measures answer quality, because evaluation is a stated responsibility.

Weeks 5-6: agents and cloud. Build a small multi-agent workflow with a framework such as LangGraph, CrewAI or ADK. Deploy it on a cloud platform with a secure API, and log tracing, latency and cost per request.

Ongoing: write a clear README for every project, and rehearse a two-minute story of what you built and what broke.

Likely Interview Topics

  • Python internals, complexity analysis and clean code.
  • Designing a RAG pipeline and choosing a vector database.
  • When to use an agent versus a plain LLM call, and ReAct-style patterns.
  • Evaluating LLM output for accuracy and safety; handling hallucination.
  • Latency, tokens per second and cost control.
  • Working with a customer whose data is messy or whose requirements are unclear.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Listing AI buzzwords with no project to prove them.
  • Applying while not in the penultimate year.
  • Showing only tutorial clones without your own evaluation or deployment.
  • Ignoring DSA preparation because the role sounds AI-focused.
  • Using third-party sites as a source; always apply only through Google's own careers page.

  • Apply through the official page and keep the confirmation.

Final Takeaway

This Google internship rewards students who have actually shipped AI work, not just studied it. If you meet the penultimate-year condition and have Python, DSA and RAG experience, apply through the official link in the Official Links section above.

Build, evaluate, deploy, and show it: that is what this role is looking for.
