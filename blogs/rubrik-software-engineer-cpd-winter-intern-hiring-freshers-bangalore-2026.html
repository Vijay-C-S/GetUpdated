Rubrik Hiring Software Engineer (CPD) Winter Intern 2027 in Bangalore

November 19, 2026 by GetUpdated Team

Rubrik is hiring Winter Interns in Bangalore for 2027 graduates

Rubrik is hiring Software Engineer (CPD) Winter Interns in Bangalore for an onsite internship running from January 2027 to May 2027. The posting is live on Rubrik's official careers page, and it is aimed at final-year students graduating in 2027.

The eligibility bar is specific: a CGPA of 8 and above, a 2027 graduation year, and "Circuital branches only" as the posting words it. There is no remote option, so you must be available in Bangalore for the whole window.

This guide covers what the role involves, who can apply, how to prepare and what to avoid. Every fact below comes from Rubrik's official posting. Anything the posting does not state is marked as not disclosed.

About Rubrik

Rubrik describes itself as the Security and AI Operations Company. Its platform sits at the intersection of data, identity and AI. It aims to enable cyber resilience and trusted AI operations for enterprises, so that organisations can recover before disruption strikes. The company is listed on the NYSE under the ticker RBRK.

For a fresher, a company like this offers exposure to large-scale distributed systems, data protection and security engineering. These are problems that rarely show up in college projects. An internship here can be a strong line on a resume.

Role Overview: What CPD Means

CPD stands for Continuous Product Development. According to the posting, CPD-SWE is the part of the engineering organisation focused on end-to-end ownership of established products. So you would work on products that already exist and have real users, rather than only on greenfield prototypes.

The posting lists these responsibilities:

  • Identify and solve bottlenecks in the software stack.
  • Design, develop, test, deploy, maintain and improve software.
  • Manage your own project priorities, deadlines and deliverables using your technical expertise.

Rubrik says it prefers to give engineers "responsibility, not tasks", with a fluid structure where people can choose projects they enjoy. Expect more autonomy than a typical classroom-style internship.

Eligibility Criteria

The posting states minimum eligibility criteria clearly. Check each one before applying:

  • Degree: Bachelor's or Master's degree, preferably in Computer Science.
  • Graduation year: 2027 graduates only.
  • CGPA: 8 and above.
  • Branch: "Circuital branches only" (the posting's wording; it does not list branches, so check with Rubrik if unsure).
  • Availability: January 2027 to May 2027, in Bangalore, onsite. No remote options.
  • Skills: Proficiency in one or more general-purpose object-oriented languages such as Java, C/C++, Scala or Python.

Check your semester clash

The internship runs through May 2027. If your final semester has mandatory classes, exams or a college-mandated internship in that period, confirm your college allows this before you apply.

Important Dates

The posting does not mention an application deadline, a test date or a result date. Rubrik hires on a rolling basis for many roles, and postings can close without notice once enough candidates are in the pipeline. Treat the lack of a date as a reason to apply early, not as permission to wait.

The only dates confirmed are the internship window: January 2027 to May 2027. Since today is in November 2026, there is little time left before the start.

Stipend and Benefits

Rubrik's official posting does not disclose the stipend, and I will not guess a figure. Salary-aggregator sites sometimes publish estimates, but those are unverified. Ask the recruiter about stipend, relocation and any pre-placement offer terms if you reach the interview stage.

What the posting does say is that interns get real responsibility and the freedom to work on projects they enjoy. That is the main benefit it advertises.

Selection Process

The posting does not describe the selection stages. For software internships at product companies of this kind, candidates commonly face an online coding assessment and one or more technical interviews. Treat this as a general expectation, not a confirmed Rubrik process.

Rubrik's own words are useful, though: it wants "self-starters" who can manage themselves and have "limitless drive". Expect interviewers to probe ownership, curiosity and how you handle ambiguity, alongside coding fundamentals.

How to Apply

  1. Open the official Rubrik posting.
  2. Read the eligibility section again and confirm CGPA, branch and 2027 graduation.
  3. Click the apply button and fill in the application form on the careers page.
  4. Upload a one-page resume with your CGPA, projects, GitHub link and languages.
  5. Submit and keep a record of the confirmation email.
  6. Check your inbox and spam folder regularly for assessment or interview invites.

Resume tip

Lead with two or three projects where you owned the design, the code and the testing. This matches what the CPD team says it values: end-to-end ownership.

Preparation Plan

Because the posting stresses object-oriented languages, pick one (Java, C++ or Python) and be comfortable in it. A realistic plan for the next few weeks:

  • Data structures and algorithms: arrays, strings, hash maps, trees, graphs, heaps, recursion and dynamic programming basics. Solve medium-level problems daily.
  • Core CS: operating systems, DBMS, computer networks and basic concurrency.
  • OOP and design: classes, interfaces, polymorphism, and simple low-level design problems.
  • Projects: be ready to explain every line and design choice in what you list.
  • Basics of distributed systems and security: a light read on caching, replication, backups and authentication helps, given Rubrik's domain.

Practise explaining your thinking aloud while coding. Interviewers weigh communication nearly as much as the final answer.

Likely Interview Topics

These are preparation suggestions, not a confirmed question list:

  • Coding problems on arrays, linked lists, trees and graphs.
  • Time and space complexity analysis of your solutions.
  • Questions about your language of choice, such as memory management, collections and exceptions.
  • Walkthrough of a project: bottlenecks you found, how you debugged and what you would improve.
  • Behavioural questions on ownership, deadlines and learning something new quickly.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Applying without meeting the CGPA 8+ or 2027 graduation criteria.
  • Ignoring the onsite, no-remote requirement and having to withdraw later.
  • Listing technologies on your resume that you cannot discuss.
  • Waiting for a deadline that is not published.
  • Relying on third-party sites for stipend or process details instead of asking Rubrik directly.
