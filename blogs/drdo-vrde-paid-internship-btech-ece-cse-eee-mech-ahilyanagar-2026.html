DRDO VRDE Paid Internship 2026 for B.E/B.Tech Students: Rs. 30,000 Stipend, Apply by 12 December

November 24, 2026 by GetUpdated Team

DRDO VRDE paid internship 2026

The Vehicles Research & Development Establishment (VRDE), a DRDO lab under the Ministry of Defence, has invited applications for a six-month paid internship for B.E/B.Tech students going into their final year in 2026-27. The stipend is Rs. 30,000 in total, paid in two instalments, and the internship starts tentatively on 1 January 2027.

Four engineering disciplines are open: Electronics & Communication, Computer Science, Electrical and Mechanical. The last date is 12 December 2026, and applications go by post with a scanned copy sent by mail. Students with backlogs are not eligible.

If you want a DRDO lab name on your resume and real project exposure before final year, this is a strong opportunity, but the offline process means you must act quickly.

About VRDE and DRDO

VRDE works under DRDO and focuses on research and development of light tracked and wheeled vehicles. According to the notice, it is also committed to indigenous solutions in mobility systems, wheeled and light tracked combat systems, and wheeled launched systems for strategic and tactical programmes.

The establishment is located at Vahannagar PO, Ahilyanagar, 414006, Maharashtra. For engineering students, a lab like this offers exposure to vehicle electronics, embedded control, power systems, mechanical design and software that supports defence platforms.

Vacancy Details

The internship has multiple seats spread across four disciplines: Electronics & Communication Engineering, Computer Science Engineering, Electrical Engineering and Mechanical Engineering. The notice calls the seat numbers indicative, which means they may increase or decrease at the discretion of the Establishment. No category-wise break-up is given.

Because seats are limited and the numbers can change, treat each application as competitive. Make sure your branch matches one of the four disciplines listed.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Student of B.E/B.Tech going into the final year in academic year 2026-2027 (currently in 7th semester, as the form refers to VII/VIII semester).
  • Discipline: ECE, CSE, Electrical or Mechanical Engineering.
  • No backlogs are allowed.
  • No work experience is required, and the notice lists no desirable qualification.
  • Age limit: not specified in the notice.

Backlogs disqualify you

Even one active backlog makes you ineligible. Check your latest marks card before spending time on the application.

Important Dates

Apply early

Applications received after 12 December 2026 will not be considered. Since the form goes by post, send it well ahead, ideally by speed post with tracking.

Stipend and Tenure

The stipend is Rs. 30,000 in total: Rs. 15,000 after completing 3 months and Rs. 15,000 after completing 6 months. It depends on a minimum attendance of 45 days in each three-month period.

VRDE will not bear lodging, boarding, transport, medical or other expenses. Students who do not complete the required three-month tenure get no payment. Only those who finish all six months, clear the project evaluation and submit the project report receive the Certificate of Completion. If you leave midway, no certificate is issued.

Budget for Ahilyanagar

Since accommodation is not provided, plan your stay and living costs before accepting. The stipend is modest, so the value lies mainly in the experience and certificate.

Application Fee

The notice does not mention any application fee. Do not pay anyone claiming to arrange an internship seat at VRDE.

Selection Process

The notice excerpt we reviewed does not describe a written test or interview. Selection is expected to be based on the applications received, with CGPA and discipline likely mattering, but this is not confirmed. Keep your documents ready in case you are contacted, and read the official notification for the final process.

How to Apply

  1. Download the notification from the Official Links section below and print the "Format for Application for Paid Internship Scheme" (page 3).
  2. Fill in name, date of birth, Aadhar number, college details, discipline, branch code, degree (UG B.E or B.Tech) and semester (7th/8th), CGPA on a 10-point scale, languages known, addresses, contact numbers and email.
  3. Paste a passport-size photograph (4.5 x 3.5 cm), tick the checklist and sign the declaration with place and date.
  4. Get the referral letter printed on your college letterhead for a VII/VIII semester engineering student.
  5. Attach the enclosures and post the application to the Director, VRDE, Vahannagar PO, Ahilyanagar - 414006, Maharashtra.
  6. Send a scanned copy of all documents by mail, as stated in the notification.

Preparation Tips

Even without a stated exam, prepare as if you may be shortlisted for a conversation. Revise core subjects of your branch and be ready to discuss your academic projects clearly.

  • ECE: embedded systems, microcontrollers, communication basics, sensors, PCB design.
  • CSE: data structures, C/C++ or Python, operating systems, basic networking.
  • Electrical: power electronics, motors and drives, control systems, batteries.
  • Mechanical: machine design, CAD, thermodynamics, vehicle dynamics basics.

Prepare a short project summary and know your CGPA, semester and branch code exactly as written on your documents.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Applying with a backlog or from a non-listed branch.
  • Missing the referral letter or the college seal and signature.
  • Forgetting to email the scanned copy after posting the hard copy.
  • Posting too late, so the application arrives after 12 December.
  • Illegible handwriting or incorrect Aadhar number.

Post early, email the scan the same day, and keep a photocopy of everything you send.

This is a good chance for pre-final year engineering students to work in a DRDO laboratory and earn a stipend and certificate. Check the notification through the Official Links section, confirm you have no backlogs, and send your application early.
