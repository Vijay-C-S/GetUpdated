C-DOT Paid Internship 2027 (Winter Batch): Rs. 50,000 Monthly Stipend for B.Tech/BE CS & EC Students

November 20, 2026 by GetUpdated Team

C-DOT Paid Internship 2027 for B.Tech/BE CS and EC students

C-DOT, the Centre for Development of Telematics, has opened applications for its Paid Internship Scheme (Winter Batch 2027). Selected B.Tech/BE students from CS and EC streams get a consolidated stipend of Rs. 50,000 per month and work on real telecom R&D at the C-DOT campuses in Delhi or Bengaluru.

The scheme is meant only for students of the top 30 engineering institutes as per NIRF Ranking (2025). The application window runs from 19 November 2026 to 30 November 2026, so you have only days left. This guide explains eligibility, documents, the process and the mistakes that get applications rejected.

Apply early

The portal closes on 30 November 2026. The notification does not print a closing time, so do not wait for the last evening. An application counts as complete only after final submission and receipt of the confirmation email.

About C-DOT

C-DOT describes itself as a premier telecom technology R&D centre of the Government of India. Its campus is at Mehrauli, New Delhi-110030. For decades it has designed telecom products and technologies in India, which makes it a rare place where a student can see research turn into deployed networks.

The objective of the paid internship programme, in the official words, is to attract young talent from the top engineering institutes for R&D work. It gives students an insight into the technical work and a possible route to future employment, although the notice does not promise a job after the internship.

Internship Overview and Vacancy Position

Interns are generally inducted twice a year, in Summer and Winter, depending on C-DOT's requirement. This notification covers the Winter batch, which starts from 11 January 2027 onwards. The notice states that the total intake per year is limited, that the final vacancy position may vary, and that offers are issued based on the final vacancy position at the time of selection.

C-DOT also limits how many interns can be taken from a single institute in a calendar year, so even a strong profile competes with peers from your own college. The scheme additionally allows a student to complete a semester at C-DOT on R&D work, supervised jointly by C-DOT and the institute.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Indian student of one of the top 30 engineering institutes in the latest NIRF Ranking (2025).
  • Studying Computer Science, Electronics & Communication, or an equivalent relevant branch (AI, Data Science, ML, IT etc.).
  • Pursuing B.Tech / BE and having completed second-year exams with results declared, so the current semester should be between 5 and 8.
  • Minimum CGPA 7.0 out of 10 or 70%, as on the last semester for which results are declared.
  • A No-Objection Certificate (NOC) from the competent authority of your institute.
  • You can apply for an internship only once during your course.

Meeting these conditions does not guarantee selection. Candidates with exposure to the area of intended internship and a good academic background get preference. The notice does not specify an age limit or an application fee.

Stipend and Benefits

Interns receive a consolidated stipend of Rs. 50,000 per month, subject to the mandatory attendance requirement. Besides the stipend, interns can use basic facilities such as the C-DOT canteen at subsidised rates.

Interns who do not complete the minimum period of three months will not be issued a certificate. So plan your semester, projects and any other commitments before applying.

Selection Process

Selection is based on eligibility, academic background and C-DOT's demand in various areas. Applications are screened first, and the shortlisted candidates are called for an interview through individual emails. Final offer letters follow, and C-DOT publishes final results on its website.

C-DOT says it will not entertain queries about the selection process or enter into correspondence about reasons for non-selection. Therefore, get the application right the first time.

How to Apply

  1. Keep documents ready: CV/resume, academic marksheets, photograph and the signed institute NOC.
  2. Open the C-DOT Paid Internship portal through the link in the Official Links section below.
  3. First-time users click Register and enter name, email, institute, degree, semester and CGPA/percentage.
  4. Verify your email with the OTP. Existing users log in with email and password.
  5. Go to Apply and fill in the internship form, including the internship start and end dates.
  6. Upload all documents. The resume is mandatory and goes under the Additional Documents section.
  7. Review every field, submit, and wait for the confirmation email.

If you need to change your mind, you can withdraw the application by logging in and choosing Withdraw Application. For help, the notice lists the support email support-paidintern@cdot.in.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Instant rejection risks

Selecting a wrong college from the drop-down, or entering wrong degree, semester, CGPA or percentage, leads to immediate rejection at any stage. An unsigned NOC also gets the application rejected.

  • Choosing a college outside the listed 30, even by mistake.
  • An NOC that does not cover the entire internship duration. Final-year students must ensure it does not go beyond the last working day of the last semester.
  • Forgetting to upload the resume under Additional Documents.
  • Submitting the form but never checking for the confirmation email.
  • Applying more than once. Only one application per candidate is allowed.

Preparation Tips

C-DOT works on telecom, networking, core and 5G related software, embedded systems and security. A sensible way to prepare is to polish a resume with two or three solid projects in your core area, whether networking, embedded C, Linux, Python, Java or ML. Be ready to explain every line of every project, because interviewers usually probe depth.

Resume tip

Since exposure in the intended area gets preference, put relevant projects, GitHub links and coursework at the top of your one-page resume, and keep claims you can defend in an interview.

Revise fundamentals: data structures, operating systems, computer networks (OSI/TCP-IP), digital electronics or signals, depending on your branch. Also read about C-DOT's work on its official website so you can say clearly why you want to intern there.

FAQs

1. What is the last date to apply for the C-DOT paid internship?

Applications for the winter batch 2027 are open from 19 November to 30 November 2026 on the C-DOT paid internship portal.

2. How much stipend does C-DOT pay?

A consolidated stipend of Rs. 50,000 per month, subject to mandatory attendance.

3. Who can apply?

Indian B.Tech/BE students of CS, EC or relevant branches from the top 30 engineering institutes in NIRF 2025, with minimum CGPA 7.0/10 or 70%, who have completed second year.

4. How long is the internship?

Minimum three months and maximum one year. No certificate is issued if the minimum period is not completed.

5. Where will the internship be held?

At C-DOT campuses in Delhi or Bengaluru.

6. Is an NOC required?

Yes. A signed No-Objection Certificate from your institute covering the entire internship period must be uploaded; unsigned NOCs are rejected.

7. Is there an application fee or age limit?

The official notification does not mention an application fee or age limit.

8. How are candidates selected?

Based on eligibility, academic background and demand; shortlisted candidates are contacted by email for an interview.
