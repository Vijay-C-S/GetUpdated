ONGC Apprentice Recruitment 2026 — 3,047 tentative seats for graduate, diploma and ITI/10th/12th pass candidates

ONGC Apprentice Recruitment 2026 is open for 3,047 tentative apprentice seats across 24 work centres of Oil and Natural Gas Corporation Limited, a Maharatna central public sector enterprise. The seats are open to 10th/12th pass, ITI, diploma and graduate candidates, so this one is for a very wide group of freshers. The advertisement is No. ONGC/APPR/1/2026, dated 29 October 2026.

The timing matters. The NATS window closes on 20 November 2026, which is tomorrow. For NAPS trades, ONGC issued a corrigendum on 19 November 2026 extending the last date to 29 November 2026 due to technical reasons. Know which scheme your trade falls under before you open a portal.

This guide is based on ONGC's official advertisement and corrigendum. It covers the sector-wise seats, trade-wise qualification, age limit, stipend, selection steps and exactly how to apply.

Official update: ONGC's Corrigendum No. ONGC/APPR/1/2026/Cor-1 dated 19 November 2026 extends only the NAPS last date to 29 November 2026. The notification's NATS last date remains 20 November 2026. The notification also says ONGC may extend the application date if it receives too few applications. Check the official portals before you plan around any date.

About ONGC

ONGC describes itself in the notification as India's flagship energy major and a Maharatna Central Public Sector Enterprise, engaged mainly in exploration and production of oil and gas in India and abroad. As a skill-building initiative, it engages apprentices from its operational areas at 24 work centres.

For a fresher, an ONGC apprenticeship means 12 months of structured, on-the-job training inside a large energy PSU. It builds real workplace skills and a strong line on the CV. It is not a job, which we cover honestly below.

Be clear about what this is

The notification says ONGC has no obligation to offer regular employment during or after the apprenticeship, and candidates are relieved from the work area after completing it. Treat this as paid training and experience, not a guaranteed job.

Age Limit

  • 18 to 24 years as on 29 November 2026, i.e. born between 29.11.2002 and 29.11.2008.
  • Upper age relaxation: 5 years for SC/ST, 3 years for OBC (Non-Creamy Layer).
  • PwBD: up to 10 years, up to 15 years for SC/ST and up to 13 years for OBC (NCL).

Other Conditions That Can Disqualify You

  • You must not have completed or be pursuing an apprenticeship under the Apprentices Act, 1961.
  • You must not have completed three years after passing the qualifying exam as on 29 November 2026.
  • Candidates with one year or more of training or job experience after the qualification are not eligible as Technician Apprentice.
  • Domicile rule: you can apply for a work centre only if you reside in, or obtained your qualification from, the eligible districts for it (Annexure 1). Chennai covers Tamil Nadu and Pondicherry; Hazira covers Surat, Navsari, Tapi, Dang and Valsad; Uran covers Maharashtra with preference to Raigad.
  • Electrician posts do not accept a Wireman trade certificate. ITI must be from SBTE/NCVT-recognised institutes. Degrees must be recognised by UGC/AICTE as applicable.

Apply for one trade at one work centre

You may select one sector, one work centre and one trade. If you apply for more than one, ONGC allocates one centre and one trade at its discretion. Choose the trade that matches your qualification exactly.

Application Fee

The advertisement does not mention any application fee. Follow the instructions on the NAPS or NATS portal, and be careful of any site or agent asking for money to "register" you for ONGC.

ONGC Apprentice 2026 Selection Process

No written exam is described. The notification lays out a merit-based process:

  1. Consolidation of applications received through the NAPS and NATS portals, sorted by trade, qualification and work centre.
  2. Scrutiny of education, age, relaxation claims and scheme eligibility. Incomplete, duplicate or ineligible applications are rejected.
  3. Provisional merit list per trade/discipline and work centre, based on marks in the qualifying examination. If marks tie, the older candidate gets preference.
  4. Shortlisting for document verification in a ratio set by ONGC. The list is published on the ONGC website and/or sent by email. Shortlisting gives no right to engagement.
  5. Document verification with originals and self-attested copies.
  6. Final merit list with reservation applied (SC/ST/OBC/EWS/PwBD as per government guidelines).
  7. Engagement formalities, including registration or contract formalities on the NAPS/NATS portal, and medical fitness as per the Apprentices Act.

Because selection is on marks, your qualifying exam percentage is your biggest asset. There is nothing to "prepare" for in the usual exam sense, so accuracy in your application matters more.

How to Apply for ONGC Apprentice 2026 (NATS and NAPS)

Applications are accepted online only. Hard copy, post, hand or any other mode is not accepted.

Step-by-Step

  1. Download the official advertisement (link below) and find your trade, work centre and scheme (NAPS or NATS).
  2. Check Annexure 1 to confirm that your district is eligible for your chosen work centre.
  3. For NAPS trades: register or log in at apprenticeshipindia.gov.in, find the ONGC apprenticeship opportunity for your trade and centre, and submit before 29 November 2026.
  4. For NATS trades: register or log in at nats.education.gov.in, find the ONGC opportunity for your discipline and centre, and submit before 20 November 2026.
  5. Fill in details exactly as on your certificates: name, date of birth, qualification, marks, category and contact details. Use an email address you check daily, because selection updates reach you by email.
  6. Submit and save the confirmation or application number for later verification.
  7. Watch for updates on ongcindia.com and ongcapprentices.ongc.co.in. Results and document verification details are published there.

NATS applicants: do it today

The NATS window closes 20 November 2026. The extension in ONGC's corrigendum covers NAPS only. Portals slow down in the last hours, so finish your application today if your trade is a NATS trade.

Documents to Keep Ready

Per the notification, you may be asked for originals and self-attested copies of:

  • 10th certificate or other date of birth proof
  • Mark sheets and certificates of the qualifying exam
  • ITI, diploma or degree certificate, as applicable to your trade
  • Caste/category certificate (SC/ST/OBC Non-Creamy Layer), if claiming reservation or relaxation
  • PwBD certificate, if applicable
  • Valid ID proof
  • NAPS/NATS registration and application details
  • A certificate from your Principal with the result declaration date, if the mark sheet does not show it

Preparation Plan

There is no exam, so preparation here means getting the application and verification right.

  • Today: confirm your trade, scheme, work centre and domicile eligibility from the PDF.
  • This week: complete portal registration, upload correct details and save the confirmation.
  • Before 15 December: build one folder with originals and self-attested copies of every document above.
  • After applying: check your email (including spam) and ongcapprentices.ongc.co.in regularly.
  • If shortlisted: revise the basics of your trade, such as safety practices, tools and your project or training work, in case you are asked about them. The notification does not describe an interview, so treat this as a precaution.

Interview and Verification Topics

The advertisement describes document verification, not an interview. Be ready to explain anything you have written in your application:

  • Your trade, ITI or college details and the year you passed
  • Why your date of birth and result date meet the age and three-year rule
  • Your domicile or the district where you earned your qualification
  • Any prior apprenticeship, training or job experience (declare it honestly)

Common Mistakes

1. Missing the NATS Date

Problem: Assuming the extension applies to everyone.

Solution: The corrigendum extends NAPS only. NATS closes 20 November 2026.

2. Choosing a Work Centre Outside Your Eligible Districts

Problem: Picking a centre because it has more seats.

Solution: Check Annexure 1. Applications from outside the eligible districts are not considered.

3. Applying After Three Years Have Passed

Problem: Ignoring the rule that you must not have completed three years after your qualifying exam as on 29 November 2026.

Solution: Check your result date first.

4. Wrong Scheme Portal

Problem: Searching NATS for a NAPS trade, or the reverse.

Solution: The advertisement marks every trade as NAPS or NATS. Use the matching portal.

5. Wrong Email or Phone

Problem: Missing shortlisting mails.

Solution: ONGC says it is not responsible for lost communication from incorrect contact details.

Final Takeaway

ONGC Apprentice Recruitment 2026 is one of the widest apprenticeship openings of the year: 3,047 seats, 24 work centres, and entry points from 10th pass to engineering graduate. It pays ₹8,200 to ₹12,300 a month for 12 months with no written exam. Selection rests on your qualifying marks and your documents.

It is training, not a permanent job, and ONGC is clear about that. If you want PSU experience, are within the district rules and meet the age limit, apply. NATS candidates must finish by 20 November 2026, and NAPS candidates by 29 November 2026.

ONGC Apprentice Recruitment 2026: Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is ONGC Apprentice Recruitment 2026?

ONGC has invited online applications to engage apprentices under the Apprentices Act, 1961, through Advertisement No. ONGC/APPR/1/2026 dated 29 October 2026, with 3,047 tentative seats across 24 work centres.

2. What is the last date to apply?

The NATS last date is 20 November 2026. For NAPS trades, a corrigendum dated 19 November 2026 extended the last date to 29 November 2026.

3. What is the age limit?

18 to 24 years as on 29 November 2026, with relaxation of 5 years for SC/ST, 3 years for OBC (NCL) and up to 10 years for PwBD (more for reserved PwBD categories).

4. What is the stipend?

₹12,300 for graduate apprentices, ₹10,900 for diploma, ₹10,560 for two-year ITI, ₹9,600 for one-year ITI and ₹8,200 for 10th/12th pass trade apprentices, per month.

5. Is there a written exam?

No written exam is described. A merit list is prepared from qualifying exam marks, followed by document verification.

6. Where do I apply?

Online only: NAPS trades at apprenticeshipindia.gov.in and NATS trades at nats.education.gov.in. Offline applications are not accepted.

7. Can I apply to any work centre?

No. You must be a resident of, or have obtained your qualification from, the eligible districts listed for that centre in Annexure 1. You can apply for one trade at one work centre.

8. Will ONGC give me a permanent job after the apprenticeship?

No. The notification says ONGC has no obligation to offer regular employment during or after the apprenticeship.
