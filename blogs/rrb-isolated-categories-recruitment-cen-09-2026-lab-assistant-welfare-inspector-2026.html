RRB Isolated Categories Recruitment 2026 (CEN 09/2026): Lab Assistant, Welfare Inspector & More for Freshers

November 27, 2026 by GetUpdated Team

RRB (Railways) Lab Assistant, Welfare Inspector & other posts 2026

The Railway Recruitment Boards have opened applications under CEN 09/2026 for Isolated Categories. This notification includes several posts that need no prior work experience. They suit 12th-pass candidates, graduates and psychology postgraduates who want a central government railway job. The last date to apply is 4 December 2026, so only a few days are left.

This article covers only the posts where experience is not mandatory: Laboratory Assistant Grade III (Chemist & Metallurgist), Staff and Welfare Inspector, Senior Publicity Inspector and Scientific Assistant (Training). The notification has other posts, such as the law posts, that need years of Bar practice. Those are not covered here.

Applications go through the official portal, rrbapply.gov.in. You can apply to only one RRB, so choose carefully before you submit.

About the Recruiting Organisation

Indian Railways is one of the largest employers in India. Recruitment to many non-technical and specialised posts is run by the Railway Recruitment Boards in regions such as Bengaluru, Chennai, Mumbai, Kolkata, Secunderabad and Chandigarh. "Isolated categories" are small, specialised posts that fall outside the big Group D and NTPC style recruitments. They are advertised in one combined notification, with the same application system and a shared selection process.

Because these posts are specialised, the applicant pool is smaller than for the large railway exams. Competition is still real, since many graduates apply for any railway post.

Role Overview

  • Laboratory Assistant Grade III (Chemist & Metallurgist): This is a lab support role in railway chemical and metallurgical laboratories. The work is testing materials, preparing samples and recording results. Physics and Chemistry in Class 12 are the base for it.
  • Staff and Welfare Inspector: This role deals with staff welfare, labour matters and personnel-related administration. The listed qualifications lean towards labour laws, HR and social welfare.
  • Senior Publicity Inspector: This role handles railway publicity, media and public communication work. Training in PR, advertising, journalism or mass communication helps.
  • Scientific Assistant (Training): This is a psychology-based role in railway training and assessment. It suits Master's graduates in psychology.

Eligibility Criteria

Qualifications must be obtained by 4 December 2026. Candidates awaiting their final result are not eligible.

  • Laboratory Assistant Grade III: Class 12 (+2) or equivalent with Physics and Chemistry.
  • Staff and Welfare Inspector: Graduation plus one of the listed qualifications. These include a diploma in Labour or Social Welfare, a diploma in Labour Laws, LLB with Labour Laws papers, a PG diploma or MBA in Personnel Management/HR, or similar.
  • Senior Publicity Inspector: A degree plus a diploma in Public Relations, Advertising, Journalism or Mass Communication. Two years of relevant experience is desirable, not mandatory.
  • Scientific Assistant (Training): A Master's degree in Psychology with at least 50% aggregate marks. Industrial, Organisational or Clinical Psychology specialisation and counselling experience are desirable.

Application Fee

  • Rs. 500: General/UR, OBC-NCL and EWS.
  • Rs. 250: SC, ST, women, PwBD, ex-servicemen, transgender, minority and EBC candidates.
  • Payment is by net banking, debit/credit card or UPI. Transaction charges are extra.
  • Candidates who appear in the CBT get a refund of Rs. 400 (Rs. 500 category) or Rs. 250 (concession categories), less bank charges.
  • Each correction attempt in the modification window costs Rs. 250, and this is non-refundable.

Selection Process and Exam Pattern

  1. Computer Based Test (CBT)
  2. Document verification, based on merit, vacancies and reservation rules
  3. Medical examination
  4. Final empanelment

The CBT has 100 questions for 100 marks in 90 minutes. Scribe users get 120 minutes. Each wrong answer carries a negative mark of 1/3.

How to Apply

  1. Go to rrbapply.gov.in and select CEN 09/2026 (Isolated Categories).
  2. New users must register. Enter your name and date of birth exactly as in the Class 10 certificate. Existing RRB account holders can log in.
  3. Choose one RRB only. Multiple applications can lead to rejection and debarment.
  4. Fill in your qualifications, community details, exam language, and post and zone preferences.
  5. Complete Aadhaar/DigiLocker verification where applicable.
  6. Capture a live photo on the portal. Do not wear spectacles, a cap or a mask.
  7. Upload your signature as JPG/JPEG, 30 to 49 KB, minimum 140 x 60 pixels, in black ink on white paper.
  8. Upload other required documents. SC/ST candidates asking for a free travel pass upload the certificate as a PDF under 400 KB.
  9. Preview and submit by 4 December, then pay the fee by 6 December 2026.
  10. Save or print the submission and payment acknowledgment.

Keep these documents ready: Class 10 certificate, Class 12 and degree/PG certificates, any diplomas, ID proof and category certificates.

Preparation Plan

Half the paper is Professional Ability, so your subject matters most. Lab Assistant aspirants should revise Class 11-12 Physics and Chemistry, especially basic lab practice and material properties. Psychology postgraduates should revise core areas such as human behaviour, assessment, learning and counselling. Welfare and publicity aspirants should revise labour laws, HR basics, and media and communication concepts.

The other 50 marks are common. Spend 30 to 40 minutes daily on arithmetic, reasoning puzzles and general science. For general awareness, read current affairs, railway basics and static GK. Take full 90-minute mocks on weekends, and learn to skip questions you are unsure about because of the negative marking.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Applying to more than one RRB. This can get all your applications rejected.
  • Giving a name or date of birth that does not match the Class 10 certificate.
  • Uploading a signature or photo outside the stated size and format limits.
  • Applying while your final result is pending. The qualification must be complete by 4 December 2026.
  • Paying the fee after 6 December, or forgetting to pay at all.
  • Not checking the medical standard for your post.

Check the final notice

Read the official CEN 09/2026 notification before submitting. It has the Annexure listing which RRB has vacancies for which post.

Fresher-friendly railway posts from Level 2 to Level 6 are open, but the window closes on 4 December 2026.

If you have a 12th with Physics and Chemistry, a graduation with a welfare or media diploma, or a Master's in Psychology, check the eligibility above today. Apply to one RRB only, double-check your uploads, and start CBT practice while the date is announced.

FAQs

1. What is the last date to apply for RRB CEN 09/2026?

The last date for online application is 4 December 2026, 11:59 PM. Fee payment is allowed until 6 December 2026.

2. Which posts here need no work experience?

Laboratory Assistant Grade III, Staff and Welfare Inspector, Senior Publicity Inspector and Scientific Assistant (Training). For Senior Publicity Inspector, 2 years of experience is only desirable.

3. What is the qualification for Lab Assistant Grade III?

Class 12 (+2) or equivalent with Physics and Chemistry. It is the only post with a 12th-level essential qualification.

4. What is the application fee?

Rs. 500 for General, OBC-NCL and EWS. Rs. 250 for SC, ST, women, PwBD, ex-servicemen, transgender, minority and EBC candidates. Part of the fee is refunded to candidates who appear in the CBT.

5. What is the age limit?

The minimum is 18 years. The maximum on 1 January 2027 is 30 for Lab Assistant Gr III, 33 for Staff & Welfare Inspector and Senior Publicity Inspector, and 35 for Scientific Assistant (Training), with category relaxations.

6. Can I apply to more than one RRB?

No. Choose one RRB only. Multiple applications can lead to rejection of all of them and debarment.

7. What is the CBT pattern?

The CBT has 100 questions, 100 marks and 90 minutes. Professional Ability has 50 questions, and General Awareness, Reasoning, Mathematics and General Science make up the rest. Each wrong answer carries a 1/3 negative mark.

8. Can final-year students apply?

No. The required qualification must be obtained by 4 December 2026, and candidates awaiting final results are not eligible.
