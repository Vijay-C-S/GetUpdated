ISRO SDSC SHAR apprentice recruitment 2026 for 400 positions

ISRO's Satish Dhawan Space Centre (SDSC) SHAR, Sriharikota has opened Apprentice Recruitment 2026 for 400 positions under the Apprentices Act, 1961. The notification, dated 6 November 2026, covers Engineering Graduate, Non-Engineering Graduate, Diploma Technician, Diploma (DCCP) and Trade apprentices, plus Vocational apprentices.

No prior work experience is needed, which makes this a good chance for 2022-or-later passouts of B.E/B.Tech, B.Sc, B.Com, B.A, diploma and ITI. Online registration closes on 26 November 2026, so only a few days remain.

Apply early

The last date for online registration is 26.11.2026. You must register on NATS or the Apprenticeship portal first and then apply on UMANG, so do not leave it to the last day.

About SDSC SHAR

Satish Dhawan Space Centre SHAR is ISRO's spaceport at Sriharikota. It handles launch operations, vehicle integration and range support for India's satellite launch programme. A year here exposes apprentices to a working aerospace environment, which is rare for freshers.

The apprenticeship is a training engagement, not a job. The notification states that apprentices have no claim to temporary, ad hoc or regular employment at SHAR, ISRO or the Department of Space after training. Treat it as a high-value year of experience for your resume and skills.

  • Only Indian citizens can apply.

Stipend

The notification lists a monthly stipend for each category:

  • Engineering Graduate Apprentice: ₹12,300
  • Non-Engineering Graduate Apprentice: ₹12,300
  • Technician (Diploma) Apprentice: ₹10,900
  • Trade Apprentice: ₹9,600
  • Vocational Apprentice: ₹9,600

The notification does not state a stipend for the DCCP diploma category in the excerpt we read, so confirm it in the PDF. Shared accommodation in SHAR residential colonies may be provided subject to availability, if required.

Selection Process

There is no written exam or interview. Selection is based on academic marks.

  1. A category-wise merit list (UR/OBC/SC/ST/EWS) is prepared for each apprenticeship category from academic marks, following Government of India reservation norms.
  2. Applicants are shortlisted according to their position in the merit list for their discipline or trade.
  3. Shortlisted applicants are called for document verification. Only those who attend and meet every requirement are considered.
  4. Selected candidates submit a medical certificate of fitness at joining and sign the apprenticeship contract.

Shortlisted candidates are not eligible for travel allowance and must arrange their own stay and food during verification. Because merit decides everything, even a small mismatch between your application and your certificates can lead to disqualification.

How to Apply

  1. Register on the NATS portal or the National Apprenticeship Promotion Scheme portal (links in the Official Links section below).
  2. Upload your degree/diploma certificate (as applicable) and valid government ID while registering. You will receive an enrolment or apprenticeship registration number.
  3. Make sure your Aadhaar is linked to your bank account and enabled for DBT.
  4. Log in to the UMANG portal, search for "SDSC SHAR Apprenticeship" and select your post.
  5. Fill in all details, upload the photo, signature and certificates in JPG/JPEG/PNG format (each up to 1 MB) and submit.

Details cannot be changed after submission, and applications by any other mode are not accepted. Save a copy of your submitted application.

No application fee is mentioned in the notification.

Preparation Plan

Since selection is merit-based, your preparation is really about getting the paperwork right and being ready for verification.

  • Keep your final marksheets, provisional or degree certificate, and the 10th certificate scanned and under 1 MB each.
  • Calculate your percentage correctly. If you have CGPA, check your university's conversion formula.
  • Keep a caste or EWS certificate ready if you are claiming reservation. EWS certificates must be valid for 2026-27.
  • Prepare originals and self-attested copies for document verification, including date-of-birth proof.

Register on NATS today

NATS profile approval and uploading can take time. Start there first, since UMANG applications need your enrolment number.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Entering marks or passing year that do not match your certificates. The notification says any discrepancy leads to disqualification.
  • Applying with a pass-out year before 2022 for graduate or diploma posts.
  • Applying if you have already done an apprenticeship or have a year of work experience.
  • Skipping the Aadhaar-bank DBT linking.
  • Uploading blurry photos or signatures, or files above 1 MB.
  • Waiting for the last day, when portals tend to slow down.

Beware of fake agents

The notification says canvassing in any form leads to disqualification, and no interim correspondence is entertained. Do not pay anyone for a "seat". Queries go only to the email in the notification.
