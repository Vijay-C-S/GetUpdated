Amazon Hiring SDE I (IIT BHU, University Talent Acquisition) for 2027 Graduates in Bengaluru

Amazon Software Development Engineer I 2026

Amazon is hiring Software Development Engineer I (SDE I) through its University Talent Acquisition team, and the role is intended for 2027 graduates only. The job is listed in Bengaluru, and the posting title is tagged "IIT BHU". Applications are open through LinkedIn Easy Apply on Amazon's own posting.

For final-year students and 2027 pass-outs in computer science and related branches, an SDE I opening at Amazon is one of the most sought-after starting points in software. The posting describes work on large distributed systems, with development cycles "measured in weeks, not years".

This guide covers what the posting says, who can apply, how to apply, and how to prepare. Anything the posting does not mention, such as salary, last date and selection rounds, is marked as not disclosed instead of guessed.

Apply early

Amazon reviews applications on a rolling basis and no closing date is given. Do not wait until the last week of your semester to apply.

About Amazon

Amazon is one of the world's largest technology companies. Its work spans e-commerce, cloud computing, devices, logistics and digital services. The posting says its focus on customers is why it is "one of the world's most beloved brands", and that customer obsession is part of the company's DNA.

In India, Amazon's engineering teams work from several cities, and this posting sits under the entity ADCI - Karnataka in Bengaluru. For freshers, joining such a team means working on products used by millions of customers, sellers and products worldwide, as the job description puts it.

Role Overview

Software Development Engineers at Amazon use cutting-edge technology to solve complex problems and see the impact of their work first-hand. The posting says the company wants people who are passionate about creating new products, features and services from scratch. They should also be able to manage ambiguity and work at a pace where releases are measured in weeks.

The role is the entry-level SDE grade, SDE I. It is meant for people starting their careers, which fits the 2027-graduate restriction in the posting. LinkedIn labels the seniority as "Mid-Senior level", but that appears to be a platform default. The text itself targets graduating students, so treat it as a fresher role.

Key Responsibilities

The posting lists these responsibilities:

  • Collaborate with experienced cross-disciplinary Amazonians to conceive, design and bring innovative products and services to market.
  • Design and build innovative technologies in a large distributed computing environment and help lead fundamental changes in the industry.
  • Create solutions to run predictions on distributed systems, with exposure to innovative technologies at incredible scale and speed.
  • Build distributed storage, index and query systems that are scalable, fault-tolerant, low cost and easy to manage and use.
  • Design and code the right solutions starting with broadly defined problems.
  • Work in an agile environment to deliver high-quality software.

In plain terms, expect to own parts of a service, write production code, take part in design discussions and learn how large systems stay reliable. Several bullets mention distributed systems, so a basic grounding in that area will help your interviews and your first months on the job.

Eligibility Criteria

Basic qualification: You must be enrolled in, or have completed, a Bachelor's degree in computer science or equivalent.

Preferred qualification: A Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Data Science, Electrical Engineering, or majors relating to these fields.

Batch restriction: The posting begins with "This role is intended for 2027 graduates only." If you graduate in any other year, this particular opening is not meant for you.

A note on the IIT BHU tag

The title reads "IIT BHU, Amazon University Talent Acquisition". This points to a campus-linked hiring drive for IIT BHU. The description does not spell out whether students from other colleges can apply. If you are not from IIT BHU, check the application page for any college restriction before you invest time in it. Amazon may also run similar drives for other campuses, so keep watching for those.

Salary and Compensation

The posting does not mention salary, joining bonus or stock components. We are not publishing any figure, because unverified numbers mislead candidates. Compensation details are normally shared by the recruiter at the offer stage, so ask for the full breakup in writing, including base pay, bonus and equity, if you reach that point.

Selection Process

The posting does not describe the rounds. It only says applications are reviewed on a rolling basis and that you can log in to your candidate portal to confirm submission or check status.

Large technology companies usually test candidates on problem solving and coding, along with behavioural questions. Amazon is well known for emphasising its Leadership Principles. Treat this as general preparation advice, not as a confirmed description of this process. Wait for the recruiter's email for the actual stages and dates.

How to Apply

  1. Open the employer's LinkedIn posting from the Official Links section below.
  2. Log in to your LinkedIn account and make sure your profile shows your degree, graduation year (2027) and key projects.
  3. Click Easy Apply and fill in the requested details carefully.
  4. Attach an updated one-page resume. Put your strongest projects, internships and coding profiles near the top.
  5. Submit the application and note the Job ID (A10612367) for reference.
  6. Log in to the Amazon candidate portal to confirm that your application was submitted, as the posting advises.
  7. Watch your email, including spam, for assessment or interview invitations.

Resume tip

Use the same keywords as the posting: distributed systems, scalable, fault-tolerant, agile, design and coding. Describe your projects with what you built, which technology you used and what the result was.

Preparation Plan

Start now, because rolling reviews can move quickly.

Data structures and algorithms

Practise arrays, strings, hash maps, linked lists, stacks, queues, trees, graphs, heaps, recursion, dynamic programming and binary search. Solve problems daily on a coding platform and aim to explain your approach aloud, not just reach the answer. Pay attention to time and space complexity.

Core computer science

Revise operating systems, DBMS and SQL, computer networks and object-oriented design. Since the role mentions distributed storage and query systems, learn the basics of:

  • how databases index and query data
  • caching and replication
  • what makes a system fault-tolerant
  • the trade-offs between consistency and availability

Low-level design basics

Be ready to design a small system with clean classes, for example a parking lot, a library system or a rate limiter. Interviewers look for clear thinking and readable code.

Behavioural preparation

Prepare six to eight real stories from projects, internships or college life. Cover ownership, handling a failure, disagreeing respectfully, learning something quickly and putting users first. Structure each story with the situation, your task, your actions and the result.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Ignoring the batch rule. Applying when you are not a 2027 graduate wastes your time.
  • Skipping the college check. The IIT BHU tag may limit who can apply, so confirm on the application page.
  • A cluttered resume that hides your best project on page two.
  • Memorising solutions instead of understanding patterns. Interviewers change the problem slightly to test this.
  • Silent coding in interviews. Explain your thinking and ask clarifying questions.
  • Weak behavioural answers with no clear result.
  • Trusting unofficial pages. Apply only through the employer's posting and Amazon's own portal, and never pay anyone for a "referral" or "confirmed seat".
