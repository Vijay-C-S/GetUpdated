Stripe Software Engineer Intern opening, Bengaluru

Stripe is hiring a Software Engineer Intern in Bengaluru, and the posting is live on Stripe's official careers page. The role is aimed at students pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's degree in computer science, math or a related discipline, and it promises real production work rather than a toy project.

Stripe describes the internship as competitive with high expectations. Interns work on projects across the stack, own problems end to end, and ship code that touches millions of businesses. If you are a CS or IT student in India who wants a global fintech name on your resume, this is worth a serious application.

This guide covers what the official posting says, who can apply, what is not disclosed (such as dates and stipend), and how to prepare so that your application stands out.

About Stripe

Stripe describes itself as a technology company focused on improving the conditions for economic growth. It builds programmable financial infrastructure that makes it easier and cheaper for businesses to start and scale. According to its careers page, more than 10 million businesses build on Stripe, from solo founders to large enterprises.

The company says it invests back into its technology at an unusual rate, ships product upgrades every day and maintains some of the most reliable APIs on the internet. It also highlights long-running work on risk and fraud infrastructure. For an intern, that means exposure to reliability, scale and correctness problems that most college projects never touch.

Role Overview: What an Intern Does

The posting says interns work on projects across Stripe's stack that directly affect how millions of businesses operate. You own problems end to end, supported by your manager and teammates, and you contribute to Stripe's codebase across domains that show how the global economy works.

Recent intern projects listed by Stripe include:

  • Expanding Stripe's Payments Foundation Model capabilities for generalized anomaly detection
  • Building more approachable fraud controls
  • Creating safer payouts
  • Developing user-facing solutions that prevent costly pricing mistakes and make merchant checkout setup easier

Responsibilities

  • Operate as a real member of the team: join discussions, decisions and technical design
  • Write software that is used in production and has meaningful impact
  • Give and receive technical feedback through code reviews and design discussions
  • Collaborate with engineers and cross-functional stakeholders and act on feedback
  • Learn quickly, communicate status clearly and build with the latest AI tooling

Eligibility Criteria

The minimum requirements on the official posting are:

  • ✅ A strong grasp of computer science fundamentals through pursuit of a Bachelor's or Master's degree in computer science, math or a related discipline
  • ✅ Familiarity with programming through side projects or classwork. Stripe works mostly in Ruby, Java, JavaScript, Go and Scala, and says new languages can be learned if fundamentals are strong
  • ✅ Experience from previous internships or multi-person projects, including open source, where you received feedback from mentors and peers
  • ✅ Ability to learn unfamiliar systems through independent research and by working with a mentor
  • ✅ Ability to use AI tools to speed up development while applying critical thinking

The page does not specify a graduation year, branch list or minimum percentage. It does mention a preference for candidates with at least one year of university education or equivalent work experience. Because batch and timing are not stated, read the posting carefully and apply only if you fit the degree requirement.

Who should apply?

B.E/B.Tech/M.Tech students in CS, IT, ECE with strong coding skills, plus math or related-discipline students who can code well. Group projects and open source contributions help a lot.

The Kind of Person Stripe Wants

Stripe's posting describes three traits. An ambitious builder enjoys solving problems without clear precedent. A rigorous thinker knows that worthwhile things are rarely simple. An adaptable problem solver treats obstacles as opportunities and takes measured risks.

In practice, show these traits with evidence: a project where you owned the design, a bug you traced across layers, or a pull request that went through review rounds. Claims without proof rarely work in a competitive pool.

Stipend and Internship Dates

The official posting does not list a stipend, internship duration or start date. It notes that Stripe does not yet include pay ranges in job postings in every country. We therefore will not quote any figure. Any number you see elsewhere is unverified, so confirm details directly with the recruiter if you are shortlisted.

Do not trust unofficial numbers

Stipend claims on Telegram groups and aggregator sites are often guesses. Treat only communication from Stripe's recruiting team as reliable.

Location and Work Setup

The role is listed for the Bengaluru office. The page says most Stripe offices expect employees to spend at least 50% of their time in the office each month, but the specific requirement for this role is not stated. Ask your recruiter about hybrid expectations and relocation if you are outside Bengaluru.

Selection Process

The posting does not outline interview rounds or timelines. Stripe only says the internship program is competitive. Based on the stated requirements, you should expect your application to be judged on coding fundamentals, project experience and your ability to learn and communicate. Do not assume a specific round structure; follow the emails you receive.

Keep your email and phone number correct on the form, and check spam folders regularly after applying.

How to Apply

  1. Open the official application through the link in the Official Links section below.
  2. Read the full posting once, including the minimum requirements.
  3. Click the apply button and fill in your personal details accurately.
  4. Upload a one-page resume with projects, internships, open source work and tech stack.
  5. Add links to GitHub or a portfolio where they show real work.
  6. Review everything and submit. Save the confirmation email.

Apply early

No closing date is disclosed, which means the posting could close at any time. Apply as soon as your resume is ready.

Preparation Plan

Because the bar is high, start with fundamentals and build upward.

  • Data structures and algorithms: arrays, hash maps, trees, graphs, heaps and dynamic programming. Practise explaining your thinking aloud.
  • One language deeply: Java, Go or JavaScript are good picks since Stripe uses them. Know its standard library well.
  • Systems basics: HTTP, REST APIs, databases, indexing, caching and concurrency.
  • Payments awareness: understand idempotency, retries, and why correctness matters when money moves.
  • Projects: build a small API with authentication, tests and documentation, and deploy it.
  • AI tooling: the posting asks for effective use of AI tools. Practise using them to speed up work while still reviewing every line critically.

Topics to Revise

Since the posting highlights code reviews and design discussions, practise reading other people's code and giving feedback. Contribute a small fix to an open source repository if you have not yet; it shows you can work with mentors and strangers.

Also revise your own projects thoroughly. Be ready to explain why you chose a design, what broke, what you would change and how you tested it.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Sending a generic resume filled with tutorial projects and no outcomes
  • Listing skills you cannot discuss in depth
  • Ignoring the stated requirement about feedback from mentors and multi-person projects
  • Waiting for a deadline that is not announced
  • Paying anyone for a "referral" or "guaranteed selection"; Stripe's application is free through its official page
