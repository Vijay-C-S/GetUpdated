About KSFES

The Karnataka State Fire and Emergency Services is the state department responsible for fire fighting, rescue and emergency response. Its work includes fires in homes, industries and commercial buildings, road accidents, floods, building collapses and other disasters. Its stations are spread across districts and taluks.

A Fire Station Officer is a first-line supervisory officer. The post leads a station's crew, manages equipment and responds to calls. It is a service where leadership, fitness and calm decision-making matter as much as academic marks.

KEA conducts many state government recruitments for Karnataka. Its portal hosts the notices and the online application forms. Candidates should create their profile there and keep all documents ready.

Role Overview

A Fire Station Officer is typically responsible for running the station and for operations at the scene. Based on how the role works in fire services generally, duties include the following. The notification gives the official duty list.

  • Leading the fire crew during firefighting and rescue operations.
  • Maintaining fire engines, pumps, breathing apparatus and other equipment.
  • Training and drilling firefighters in rescue, first aid and safety.
  • Inspecting buildings for fire safety and advising on prevention.
  • Keeping station records, duty rosters and incident reports.

The Chemistry requirement fits the work. Fires involve combustion, hazardous chemicals, gases and fire extinguishing agents, so a chemistry background helps in understanding how fires behave and how to control them.

How to Apply

  1. Open the official notification through the link in the Official Links section below and read it fully.
  2. Go to the KEA recruitment portal and find the Fire Station Officer link for KSFES.
  3. Register or log in with your mobile number and email.
  4. Fill in personal, educational and category details exactly as in your certificates.
  5. Upload the photo, signature and documents in the specified size and format.
  6. Pay the fee, if applicable, through the online gateway.
  7. Submit the form, then save and print the confirmation for later use.

Keep these ready before you start: SSLC/10th marks card for date of birth, B.Sc marks cards and degree certificate, caste and income certificates if claiming reservation, Kannada medium or rural certificates if relevant, and a recent passport photo.

Preparation Plan

Start now, because the physical and written parts both need time.

Physical fitness

  • Run daily, building from a steady jog to timed runs.
  • Add push-ups, pull-ups, sit-ups, and rope or ladder climbing practice, since fire service tests often use such tasks.
  • Work on stamina and grip strength. Fire fighting is physically demanding.
  • Avoid injuries by increasing effort gradually.

Written test

  • Revise B.Sc Chemistry: organic, inorganic and physical chemistry, with attention to combustion, gases, acids and bases.
  • Prepare general knowledge, current affairs, Karnataka geography and history, and basic reasoning and maths.
  • Learn the basics of fire safety, fire types, extinguishers and disaster management.
  • Practice previous papers of KEA and other Karnataka government exams, and take timed mock tests.
