Stripe Hiring Software Engineer, New Grad in Bangalore 2026: Eligibility, Prep & How to Apply

November 21, 2026 by GetUpdated Team

Stripe is hiring new grad Software Engineers in Bangalore

Stripe is hiring a Software Engineer, New Grad for its Bangalore office, and the opening is aimed at students and recent graduates in computer science who will finish their degree by summer 2027. If you are in your final year, or graduated recently with under 18 months of full-time experience, this is one of the most sought-after fresher roles in the fintech world.

Stripe builds programmable financial infrastructure used by more than 10 million businesses. A new grad engineer here works on real production systems from the early days, with mentors and small teams. This guide covers the eligibility, the skills Stripe lists, a realistic preparation plan, and mistakes to avoid before you apply.

The official posting does not publish a closing date in the text we reviewed, so treat the role as open but could close at any time. Apply early.

About Stripe

Stripe describes itself as a technology company focused on improving the conditions for economic growth. It builds financial infrastructure, mainly payments and related tools, that makes it easier and cheaper for any business to start and scale. Customers range from solo founders to large enterprises, and Stripe says they process trillions of dollars a year on the platform.

The company says it invests back into its technology at an unusual rate, ships product upgrades every day, and maintains some of the most reliable APIs on the internet. It also highlights its work in risk and fraud infrastructure. For a fresher, this means you will learn what reliability, security and correctness mean when real money is involved.

Role Overview

Stripe says it is large enough that projects can reach a meaningful fraction of the world's businesses, yet small enough that individual agency has an outsized impact. The posting also says the global economy is replatforming around AI, and Stripe wants people who move with urgency, like hard problems and want their work to matter.

The posting is generic about daily tasks, so do not expect a fixed list of duties. Typically, a new grad software engineer would write code, review code, learn unfamiliar systems with a mentor, and ship features in a small team. Stripe stresses learning unfamiliar systems through independent research and help from subject matter experts.

What "new grad" means here

Stripe defines new grads as candidates with no more than 18 months of professional experience, excluding internships. Final-year students and recent graduates both fit, provided the degree is obtained by summer 2027.

Eligibility Criteria

These are the minimum requirements listed on the official posting:

  • A Bachelor's or Master's degree in computer science or a directly related field, obtained by summer 2027, or equivalent work experience.
  • No more than 18 months of professional experience, excluding internships.
  • Familiarity with programming through side projects or classwork.
  • Experience from internships or from working collaboratively on multi-person coding projects, at university or professionally.
  • Ability to learn unfamiliar systems through independent research and with mentors.
  • Clear written communication to explain your work to stakeholders and teammates.
  • Ability to use AI tools to speed up development while critically reviewing and validating every output.

Preferred, but not mandatory: specialised knowledge in frontend, backend or infrastructure; experience with code review and safely updating production systems; comfort working in large code bases; and leading small projects independently. A good ECE or IT candidate with strong programming projects can still make a credible case.

Stripe cares about fundamentals and collaboration more than any single language.

Skills Stripe Looks For

Stripe works mostly in Java, Ruby, JavaScript, Scala and Go, but the posting clearly says new languages can be learned if the fundamentals are present. So do not worry if you only know one or two of these. Be strong in data structures, algorithms, object-oriented design, and at least one language from the list or a close cousin like C++ or Python.

The posting also describes the traits it values: an ambitious builder who is curious, a rigorous thinker who enjoys problems not tackled before, and an adaptable problem solver. Stripe says it admires ambition, intensity, curiosity, humility and rigor. Prepare examples from your own projects that show these, because interviewers usually probe them.

Salary and Compensation

The official posting text we reviewed does not state a salary or stipend for the Bangalore role. We will not guess a figure. Check the compensation or pay range section on the live posting, if shown, and ask the recruiter during the process. Be wary of unofficial salary numbers on social media.

Selection Process

The posting does not describe the interview stages, so what follows is general guidance and not an official Stripe schedule. Software engineering hiring at product companies generally includes a recruiter conversation, one or more technical coding rounds, and a round on practical engineering or collaboration. Stripe's emphasis on code review, working with large code bases and AI-assisted development suggests you may be asked to reason about realistic code, not only puzzles.

Whatever the format, confirm the stages with your recruiter once you are contacted, and follow only instructions sent from official Stripe email addresses.

How to Apply

  1. Open the official Stripe job posting using the link in the Official Links section below.
  2. Read the full description and confirm that Bangalore and the new grad criteria suit you.
  3. Update your resume to one page, with projects, internships, languages and links to GitHub.
  4. Fill in the application form with accurate details, matching your resume.
  5. Upload your resume and any other documents the form asks for.
  6. Submit, and save the confirmation email for future reference.

Apply early

No closing date is published. Global companies often close new grad postings once enough applications arrive, so submit as soon as your resume is ready.

Preparation Plan

If you have a few weeks, split your time like this:

  • Weeks 1-2, fundamentals: arrays, strings, hash maps, trees, graphs, recursion, sorting and complexity analysis. Solve problems in your strongest language.
  • Weeks 3-4, practical coding: build or polish a project with an API, a database and tests. Practise reading someone else's code and fixing a bug.
  • Week 5, systems basics: HTTP, REST APIs, databases, caching, idempotency and retries. These matter in payments.
  • Week 6, communication: practise explaining your approach aloud and writing short clear design notes.

Use AI wisely

Stripe expects you to use AI tools to move faster while checking every output. Be ready to show that you can review, test and correct generated code instead of trusting it blindly.

Interview Topics to Revise

  • Data structures and algorithms with clean, working code.
  • Object-oriented design and writing readable, testable functions.
  • Debugging: reading a failing test or log and finding the cause.
  • Basics of web services, APIs, databases and concurrency.
  • Your projects: why you chose a design, what broke, what you learned.
  • Teamwork: how you handled disagreement or review feedback in a group project.

Since Stripe's customers depend on it for their livelihoods, expect questions that reward care with edge cases, error handling and correctness.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Applying with a long, generic resume that does not highlight collaborative coding projects.
  • Ignoring the graduation window: the degree should be obtained by summer 2027, or you need equivalent work experience.
  • Claiming skills in Java, Go or Scala that you cannot discuss in depth.
  • Rushing to code without clarifying the problem and edge cases.
  • Trusting unofficial job groups or agents. Stripe does not need you to pay for an application.

Stay safe

Apply only through the official Stripe careers link. Never pay money to anyone claiming they can get you a Stripe job.

This is a strong opportunity for CS freshers in India who enjoy building and want to learn how a world-class payments company works. Stripe cares about fundamentals, curiosity, clear writing and collaboration, so prepare your projects and basics well.
