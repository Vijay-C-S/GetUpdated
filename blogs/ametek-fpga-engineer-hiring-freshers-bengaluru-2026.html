AMETEK FPGA Engineer 2026

AMETEK is hiring an FPGA Engineer in Bengaluru, and the role is open to entry-level candidates. The posting was live one day before this article was written, and applications run through LinkedIn Easy Apply. No last date is given, so treat it as a role to apply for soon rather than something to leave for later.

About AMETEK

AMETEK, Inc. describes itself as a leading global provider of industrial technology solutions serving niche markets, with annual sales over $7.5 billion. The company employs about 22,000 colleagues in 35 countries and is a component of the S&P 500.

Its stated mission is to make a safer, sustainable and more productive world a reality. Its core values are Ethics and Integrity, Respect for the Individual, Inclusion, Teamwork and Social Responsibility. For a fresher, a large engineering-led manufacturer like this usually means structured processes, documented design practices and exposure to products that must meet strict quality requirements.

Role Overview: What You Will Do

The posting lists several responsibilities, and together they show how broad a first FPGA role here can be.

  • Take part in the complete FPGA/CPLD design cycle for embedded data acquisition applications, from requirements analysis to validation.
  • Develop designs in Verilog, VHDL and SystemVerilog.
  • Work with hardware design engineers on system-level architecture, board-level design, hardware validation and integration.
  • Run simulation, synthesis, implementation, timing analysis and hardware bring-up.
  • Support development and verification of FPGA IP integration, including processor, communication and memory interfaces.
  • Make sure designs meet functional, performance and quality requirements under the Engineering Development Process.
  • Prepare and maintain technical documentation, design reports and test reports.
  • Provide sustaining engineering support for existing products and FPGA designs.

Two points stand out. Documentation and sustaining engineering are part of the job, so clear writing and patience with existing code matter as much as writing new RTL. You will also work closely with hardware engineers, so understanding schematics and board behaviour will help you.

Eligibility Criteria

Check each point below before you apply:

  • BE/B.Tech in Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE), Electrical & Electronics Engineering (EEE), Computer Science, or a related discipline.
  • 0-2 years of experience in FPGA/CPLD design and development. Freshers fall inside this range.
  • Basic understanding of FPGA architecture, digital design, finite state machines (FSMs), synchronous design techniques and clock domain crossing.
  • Working knowledge of Verilog, VHDL and SystemVerilog.
  • Familiarity with Xilinx Vivado and Vitis.
  • Understanding of common IP cores and interfaces: I2C, SPI, UART, AXI, DDR memory, PCIe, Ethernet and high-speed SerDes.
  • Ability to read synthesis and implementation reports, do timing analysis, and write timing and pin constraints.
  • Strong analytical, debugging and problem-solving skills, with good communication and technical reporting skills.

Desirable and added-advantage skills

  • A Master's degree in VLSI design.
  • Soft processor and embedded system integration in FPGA designs.
  • Simulation, testbench development and verification methodologies.
  • Basic TCL scripting.
  • Knowledge of high-speed data acquisition, data processing and storage architectures.

The posting also expects a demonstrated willingness to use AI tools to improve productivity and work quality, and to judge AI-generated output critically. Be ready to give an example of how you have used AI tools in coding or debugging.

Fresher angle

You do not need to tick every desirable item. Strong fundamentals in digital design and FSMs, plus one or two solid projects in Vivado, can carry an entry-level application.

Preparation Plan for Freshers

Spend a focused week or two on the topics the posting names.

Digital design basics

  • Revise combinational and sequential logic, setup and hold time, and metastability.
  • Practise writing Moore and Mealy FSMs in Verilog or VHDL and explain your state encoding choices.
  • Understand clock domain crossing: two-flop synchronisers, handshake methods and asynchronous FIFOs.

Tools and flow

  • Know the flow: simulation, synthesis, implementation, bitstream generation and hardware bring-up.
  • Learn to read timing reports and spot failing paths, and know what timing and pin constraints do.
  • Try a small design in Vivado and, if you can, a soft-processor design using Vitis.

Interfaces

  • Be able to explain I2C, SPI and UART in simple terms, including clocking and framing.
  • Study the basics of AXI, and know what DDR memory, PCIe, Ethernet and SerDes are used for.

Scripting and verification

  • Learn simple TCL scripts for Vivado, such as batch builds.
  • Write a basic testbench with stimulus and self-checking, since verification skills are a stated plus.

Likely Interview Topics

These are preparation suggestions based on the listed skills, not confirmed questions.

  • Difference between blocking and non-blocking assignments, and between latches and flip-flops.
  • How you would fix a timing violation.
  • Explaining a project end to end: problem, architecture, simulation, and how you debugged it on hardware.
  • What happens during synthesis versus implementation.
  • How you would design a simple SPI or UART controller.
  • How you have used AI tools to speed up coding or debugging, and how you checked the output.

Project tip

One well-explained project, such as a UART controller, an SPI master or a small data-capture design with a testbench, is worth more than a long list of tools you have only read about.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Listing skills you cannot explain. Interviewers for FPGA roles often go deep on one claimed topic.
  • Ignoring timing analysis. The role names it explicitly, so know how to read a timing report.
  • Sending a resume that hides digital design work under unrelated projects.
  • Skipping documentation skills. Reports and test reports are part of the job.
  • Applying once and waiting. Follow up through LinkedIn and watch for recruiter messages.
  • Assuming a compensation figure. The posting only says "Market".
