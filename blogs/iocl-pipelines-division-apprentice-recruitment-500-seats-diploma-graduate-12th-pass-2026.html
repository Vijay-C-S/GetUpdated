IOCL Pipelines Division Apprentice Recruitment 2026: 500 Seats for Diploma, Graduate and 12th Pass

November 23, 2026 by GetUpdated Team

IOCL Pipelines Division apprentice recruitment 2026 for freshers

IOCL Pipelines Division Apprentice Recruitment 2026 is open for around 500 apprentice seats across India under the Apprentices Act, 1961. Indian Oil Corporation Limited (Pipelines Division) published Advertisement No. PL/HR/ESTB/APPR (2026) dated 06.11.2026, inviting diploma holders, graduates and 12th pass candidates for 12 months of paid apprenticeship training.

The seats are spread over five regions (Eastern, Western, Northern, Southern and South Eastern Pipelines) and cover Technician Apprentice (Mechanical, Electrical, Telecommunication & Instrumentation), Trade Apprentice (Assistant-Human Resource, Accountant) and Data Entry Operator trades. Age limit is 18 to 24 years as on 30.11.2026, and selection is merit based on marks in the qualifying course.

Note on the deadline: the part of the official notice we could read did not show the closing date. Secondary sites report 5 December 2026, but please confirm it in the official notice before you apply.

About IOCL Pipelines Division

Indian Oil Corporation Limited is a Government of India undertaking and, in its own words, the largest commercial enterprise in India and a Fortune Global 500 company. The Pipelines Division runs the cross-country crude oil, petroleum product and gas pipeline network that moves fuel from refineries and ports to terminals and depots.

Apprenticeship here is a skill-building programme, not a regular job. You train on the job at pipeline stations, terminals and offices, which gives freshers a real industrial work record before applying to PSUs or private companies. The notice does not promise employment after training, so treat it as experience and a stepping stone.

  • You must have already passed the qualification by the cut-off date of 30.11.2026. Result-awaited candidates are not eligible.
  • Diploma and degree must be regular full-time courses with at least 50% aggregate marks (45% for SC/ST and PwBD for posts reserved for them). Part-time, correspondence or distance degrees are not accepted.
  • Candidates with higher professional qualifications such as BE/B.Tech, MBA, CA, LLB or MCA, or pursuing them, are not eligible.
  • Age 18 to 24 years as on 30.11.2026. Upper age relaxation: 5 years SC/ST, 3 years OBC (NCL), 10 years PwBD (plus 5 or 3 years more for SC/ST or OBC PwBD).
  • The notice says apprentices are engaged from candidates registered on the NAPS/NATS portal.

B.E/B.Tech not eligible

Engineering graduates cannot apply under this notice. The technician trades are for diploma holders, and BE/B.Tech holders fall under the excluded higher qualification category.

Apply early

Do not wait for the last day. Portals slow down near deadlines, and you need to upload certificates and a percentage conversion if your marks are in CGPA.

Stipend

The notice says stipend is paid monthly as prescribed under the Apprentices Act, 1961/1973, the Apprentices Rules 1992/2019 as amended, and the Corporation's guidelines. No fixed rupee amount appears in the portion we read, so we are not quoting one. Check the notice or the portal for the exact stipend for your trade.

Training duration is 12 months from the date of engagement.

Reservation and Relaxations

Seats are reserved for SC/ST/OBC (Non-Creamy Layer)/PwBD/EWS as per applicable Government guidelines. The minimum qualifying marks are relaxed by 5% for SC/ST/PwBD candidates considered against reserved positions.

OBC candidates must make sure their caste is in the Central List of OBCs of their state and that the non-creamy layer certificate is valid on 30.11.2026. PwBD candidates (minimum 40% disability) can apply only for trades suitable for their disability type listed in the notice, and the certificate must be in Form V, VI or VII format under the RPwD Rules, 2017.

Selection Process

There is no written exam mentioned in the readable part of the notice. IOCL prepares a panel-cum-merit list for each post from candidates who applied and meet the eligibility, ranked by percentage of marks (descending) in the essential qualification for the trade applied.

  • Aggregate marks of all subjects across all years or semesters are used.
  • If you have CGPA/OGPA or grades, state the equivalent percentage in the application and carry a certificate from your board/university/institute on the conversion.
  • If your institute has no conversion formula, carry a certificate saying so. The percentage is then the total of grade scores divided by the number of semesters or years.
  • Without the required certificate at document verification, candidature will not be considered.

Your marks are your rank: a clean, consistent percentage and complete documents matter more than anything else.

How to Apply

  1. Read the full official notification from the Official Links section below, including the pages on dates and procedure.
  2. Check that you meet qualification, 50% marks, age and the 30.11.2026 cut-off.
  3. Register or update your profile on the NAPS (for ITI/Graduate/12th) or NATS (for diploma and graduate) portal as the notice directs.
  4. Apply through the official IOCL Pipelines apprentice portal given in the Official Links section, and pick the trade matching your qualification.
  5. Fill in your marks percentage carefully and upload scanned certificates, mark sheets, category and disability certificates if applicable.
  6. Submit and save the acknowledgement or application number.

Preparation Plan

Since selection is merit based, preparation is about documents and readiness for verification. Keep your original mark sheets of all semesters, provisional or final diploma/degree certificate, Aadhaar, category certificate and photographs ready. Arrange the CGPA-to-percentage certificate from your institute in advance, as colleges can take days to issue it.

If you are shortlisted, expect questions about your core subjects at document verification or onboarding. Technician trades should revise basics of machines, pumps, motors, switchgear, sensors and control instruments. Commerce and HR candidates should revise basic accounting entries and office tools, and DEO candidates should practise typing and MS Office.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Applying for a trade that does not match your diploma or degree discipline.
  • Applying with a distance or part-time degree, or with results still awaited.
  • Being a BE/B.Tech, MBA or other higher-qualified candidate.
  • Entering an incorrect percentage or not carrying the CGPA conversion certificate.
  • Using an OBC certificate that is not valid on 30.11.2026 or whose caste is not in the Central List.
  • Relying on a third-party site for the last date rather than the official notice.

This is a good, paid, 12-month training chance at a Fortune Global 500 PSU for diploma holders, commerce and arts graduates and 12th pass candidates.
