About Deltek

Deltek describes itself as the recognized global standard for project-based businesses, delivering software and information solutions that help organizations achieve their purpose. Its platform serves industries such as architecture and engineering, consulting, aerospace and defense, construction and government contracting.

The company's careers page says it has been named one of America's Best Midsize Employers by Forbes, a Best Place to Work by Glassdoor, a Top Workplace by The Washington Post and a Best Place to Work in Asia by World HRD Congress. Its homepage also highlights an AI orchestrator called Dela, which hints at the AI-heavy direction of the engineering work.

Role Overview: What You Will Do

The Associate Software Engineer contributes to the development and evolution of enterprise software. Responsibilities listed on the official page include:

  • Building and enhancing APIs, user experiences, backend services, databases, automation and business workflows.
  • Working across desktop, client/server, n-tier, on-premises, SaaS and cloud-based environments.
  • Contributing across the software development lifecycle with AI-assisted workflows and modern engineering practices.
  • Using AI-assisted tools to plan, implement, test, review and improve software.
  • Participating in code reviews, automated testing and CI/CD pipelines.
  • Collaborating with product, UX, QA and engineering teams to understand customer problems.
  • Exploring AI capabilities such as automation, intelligent insights, copilots, agentic workflows and natural language interfaces.

For a fresher this is a broad brief. You are not boxed into one narrow task; you will touch backend, data and quality practices, which is a strong learning ground for the first job.

Eligibility and Skills Required

The posting does not state a minimum degree, branch or graduation batch. Treat that as "not disclosed" rather than "open to all", and check the page again before applying. The skills it does list are:

  • Experience with one or more object-oriented languages such as .NET, Java, JavaScript or similar, gained through academic projects, internships, personal projects or other practical work.
  • Understanding of application architecture, APIs, databases, software design principles and enterprise application development.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, curiosity and initiative.
  • Experience using GenAI or AI-assisted tools for coding, testing, debugging, documentation, design or learning.
  • A growth mindset and the ability to pick up new technologies quickly.
  • Strong communication and collaboration skills.

Projects count

Deltek explicitly says experience can come from academic projects, internships or personal projects. A well-documented GitHub project can carry real weight here.

Preparation Plan

Spend a week on fundamentals before you apply or interview. Pick one language, ideally Java, C# or JavaScript, and be comfortable with classes, inheritance, interfaces, collections and exception handling.

  • OOP and design: encapsulation, polymorphism, SOLID principles and common patterns.
  • APIs: build a small REST service with CRUD operations, validation and error handling.
  • Databases: joins, indexes, normalization and simple query tuning.
  • Quality: write unit tests and learn how a basic CI/CD pipeline runs them.
  • AI tools: practise using an assistant to write, review and debug code, and be able to explain where you corrected its output.

Also practise explaining your projects in two minutes: the problem, your design choices, what went wrong and what you would improve.

Interview Topics to Expect

Because the posting emphasises enterprise software, interviewers may ask how you would structure a layered application, how you would design an API for a business workflow, or how you would find a slow database query. Basic data structures and problem-solving questions are also common for associate engineer roles.

Expect a behavioural segment too. The posting stresses communication, collaboration and a growth mindset, so prepare short stories about teamwork, learning something quickly and handling feedback in code reviews.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Applying with a generic resume that lists no projects or code links.
  • Claiming AI-tool experience you cannot demonstrate in a conversation.
  • Assuming a salary or employment type that the posting does not state.
  • Waiting for a deadline that is not published.
  • Applying through third-party sites instead of the official careers page.

Beware of fees

Legitimate employers do not charge candidates for applications or interviews. Deal only with emails from official Deltek domains.
