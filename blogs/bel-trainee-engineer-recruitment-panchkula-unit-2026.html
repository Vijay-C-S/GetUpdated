BEL Trainee Engineer Recruitment 2026: Apply Online for Panchkula Unit (Electronics, Mechanical, Electrical)

November 26, 2026 by GetUpdated Team

Bharat Electronics Limited Trainee Engineer 2026

Bharat Electronics Limited (BEL), the Navaratna defence electronics PSU, has opened applications for Trainee Engineer posts at its Panchkula Unit under Advertisement No. 2026-27/11/PK/TE/006. The notice was released on 25 November 2026, and the last date to apply online is 10 December 2026.

This is a good opening for engineering freshers. The notice lists experience as Nil for all three disciplines (Electronics, Mechanical and Electrical). Pay starts at Rs 40,000 per month consolidated in the first year and rises to Rs 50,000 by the third year.

Below you will find eligibility, age relaxation, pay, fee, dates, a step-by-step application guide and preparation tips. Read the official notification once before you submit, because BEL rejects applications for small mismatches in discipline and documents.

About Bharat Electronics Limited

BEL is a Navaratna company and a Government of India enterprise under the Ministry of Defence. The notice describes it as India's premier professional electronics company, with over 350 products. These cover military communication, radars, naval systems, C4I systems, weapon systems, homeland security, telecom and broadcast, electronic warfare, tank electronics and electro optics.

For a fresher, this means project exposure in real defence and strategic electronics. Even a contract-style trainee role at BEL looks strong on a CV, and it can lead to Project Engineer-I as explained in the pay section.

This recruitment is specifically for the Panchkula Unit, so your posting is tied to that location.

Vacancy Details

The notice invites applications for Trainee Engineer posts in three disciplines:

  • Trainee Engineer (Electronics)
  • Trainee Engineer (Mechanical)
  • Trainee Engineer (Electrical)

Posts are spread across UR, EWS, OBC, SC and ST categories, and the exact category-wise split is in the official notification. The number of posts may vary based on the actual requirement at the time of selection.

Electronics has the largest share of posts, followed by Mechanical, while Electrical has only a single post. Electrical candidates face the most competition per post, so apply with a strong profile and do not rely on a last-minute attempt.

Eligibility Criteria

Only Indian citizens can apply. The cut-off date for deciding maximum age and qualification is 01 November 2026.

Educational qualification

You need a 4-year B.E./B.Tech./B.Sc. Engineering degree from an AICTE-approved college/institute or a recognised university, with pass class, in the relevant discipline:

PostAccepted discipline
Trainee Engineer – ElectronicsElectronics / Electronics & Communication / Electronics & Telecommunication / Telecommunication / Communication
Trainee Engineer – MechanicalMechanical
Trainee Engineer – ElectricalElectrical / Electrical & Electronics

Important: if the discipline or specialisation on your degree certificate does not tally with the prescribed discipline, you will not be considered. CSE, IT and other branches are not listed in the text we reviewed, so check the official notification before applying.

  • Experience required: Nil for all three posts.
  • If your university uses CGPA or credits, attach the university's formula for converting it to percentage, plus proof of percentage and class.
  • Attach the provisional or final degree certificate showing class and percentage of marks.
  • Trainee Engineers/Officers currently working in BEL are not eligible.

Age limit and relaxation

The maximum age is 28 years for General/EWS candidates as on 01.11.2026, for all three posts.

Salary and Tenure

The remuneration is consolidated and the same for all three disciplines:

DesignationYearConsolidated pay per month
TE-I1st yearRs 40,000
TE-II2nd yearRs 45,000
TE-III3rd yearRs 50,000

Additional benefits of Rs 12,000 per year are payable towards insurance premium, attire allowance, stitching charges, footwear allowance and similar items.

Trainee Engineer-I is engaged initially for two years, which may be extended by one more year depending on project requirement and individual performance. After two years, and depending on organisational requirement, a Trainee Engineer may be advanced to Project Engineer-I if the aggregate performance score over both years is 80% or above and attendance in the preceding year is above 80%. The notice text we reviewed was cut off at this point, so read the full terms in the official PDF.

This is a fixed-term engagement with a clear growth path, not a permanent post.

Application Fee

  • Fee: Rs 150 + 18% GST
  • Exempted: SC, ST, PwBD and ex-servicemen

Pay only through the payment option on the official application portal. Keep the transaction receipt, because you may need it if the payment status is not updated.

How to Apply

  1. Read the official notification PDF fully and confirm your discipline, age and degree class.
  2. Go to the official BEL website and open the Trainee Engineer (Panchkula Unit) application link from the Official Links section below.
  3. Register with a valid email ID and mobile number that you will check regularly.
  4. Fill in personal, educational and category details exactly as per your certificates.
  5. Upload scanned documents: degree certificate, date of birth proof, category/relaxation certificate if any, and the CGPA-to-percentage conversion proof if your university uses CGPA.
  6. Pay the fee of Rs 150 + 18% GST online, unless you are in an exempted category.
  7. Review everything, submit, and save the final application PDF and payment receipt.

Applications are accepted online only, so do not send hard copies unless the official notice asks for them.

Preparation Plan

Since the selection stages are not in the text we reviewed, start with core fundamentals for your branch, which are useful in any BEL-style technical round. This is general advice, not a stated syllabus.

  • Electronics/ECE: analog and digital electronics, network theory, signals and systems, communication basics, microprocessors, and basic EMI/EMC and RF concepts.
  • Mechanical: engineering mechanics, thermodynamics, manufacturing processes, strength of materials, machine design and basics of CAD.
  • Electrical/EEE: circuit theory, machines, power systems, control systems and measurements.
  • General: aptitude, English comprehension, and current awareness about BEL products and defence electronics.

Revise your final-year project and internship in detail, because interviewers for engineering roles often start from them. Prepare a short, honest explanation of why you want to work in defence electronics.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Applying in the wrong discipline. A mismatch between your degree and the prescribed discipline leads to rejection.
  • Ignoring the 01.11.2026 cut-off date for age and qualification.
  • Skipping the CGPA-to-percentage conversion proof when your university uses CGPA.
  • Uploading unclear scans, or a certificate without class and percentage.
  • Claiming age relaxation without attaching the category certificate.
  • Using a temporary email or an unused mobile number.
  • Waiting for the last day, when the portal may be slow.
