RapidClaims Hiring Freshers 2026: SDE Intern (6 Months, On-site Bengaluru)

RapidClaims SDE Intern internship for final-year students in Bengaluru

RapidClaims is hiring SDE Interns in Bengaluru for a 6-month, on-site internship focused on backend engineering. The role is open to final-year Bachelor's and Master's students in Computer Science, IT, Software Engineering or related fields, and applications are taken through LinkedIn Easy Apply.

RapidClaims builds AI-driven revenue cycle management tools for healthcare. If you like Python, Node.js or Go, enjoy building APIs, and want to work on a live product that uses large language models, this is a strong early-career opportunity. The LinkedIn posting was shown as recently published when we checked, so apply soon.

Final-year students only: 6 months, on-site in Bangalore, backend focus (Python / Node.js / Go), apply via LinkedIn Easy Apply.

About RapidClaims

RapidClaims describes itself as a leader in AI-driven revenue cycle management. Its platform transforms medical coding and revenue operations using technology. According to the posting, the company has raised $11 million in total funding from investors including Accel and Together Fund.

The company runs a cloud-native platform with transformer-based Large Language Models fine-tuned on millions of clinical notes and claims every month. These models power autonomous coding pipelines across ICD-10-CM, CPT and HCPCS code sets, with sub-second latency and high accuracy. For an intern, that means exposure to a real production system where speed and correctness both matter.

Role Overview

RapidClaims wants Software Development Engineer Interns who are passionate about software development, backend engineering and solving real-world technical problems. You will work closely with the engineering team to design, develop and maintain scalable backend systems for the healthcare technology platform.

The internship promises hands-on exposure to building APIs, working with databases, understanding distributed systems and contributing to live product development. It is not a shadowing role; the responsibilities listed involve writing and shipping code.

Key Responsibilities

  • Assist in designing, developing and maintaining scalable backend applications and services.
  • Build and maintain RESTful APIs and support integration across system components.
  • Work with SQL and NoSQL databases to develop, manage and optimise data operations.
  • Contribute to backend development in Python, Node.js or Go.
  • Understand and work with microservices-based architectures and distributed systems.
  • Write clean, maintainable, well-tested code following engineering best practices.
  • Debug issues, troubleshoot applications and find performance improvements.
  • Take part in code reviews and collaborate with engineers, product managers and other stakeholders.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Currently pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Software Engineering or a related field.
  • Must be in the final year of graduation or post-graduation.
  • Strong understanding of programming fundamentals and Data Structures and Algorithms (DSA).
  • Basic to intermediate knowledge of at least one of Python, Node.js or Go.
  • Understanding of database concepts, including SQL and NoSQL.
  • Familiarity with REST APIs and backend fundamentals.
  • Good problem-solving and analytical skills, and a strong willingness to learn.

Good to have: frameworks such as FastAPI, Django or Express.js; exposure to Git, GitHub, Docker, Kafka or AWS; academic, personal or open-source projects; and interest in AI, Machine Learning and LLMs.

Final year only

The posting clearly says candidates must be in their final year. First- or second-year students, and those who have already graduated, do not fit the stated criteria, so check your status before applying.

What You Will Learn

The posting lists several learning outcomes: building and maintaining production-level backend systems, designing APIs and working with microservices, database management and query optimisation, software engineering best practices including code reviews and debugging, and exposure to cloud technologies, distributed systems and AI-powered healthcare applications.

You would be working in a fast-paced engineering environment alongside experienced developers. For a fresher, a 6-month stint like this can add a strong line to your resume, especially if you can speak about the APIs and database work you actually shipped.

Stipend and Duration

The internship runs for 6 months and is on-site in Bangalore. The LinkedIn posting does not disclose the stipend, and it does not mention whether a pre-placement or full-time offer is possible. Ask about both politely if you reach the interview stage.

Because it is on-site, plan for accommodation or commute within Bengaluru before you accept. Also check with your college about internship rules during your final semester, since some universities need approval letters.

Selection Process

The posting does not describe the selection stages. Based on the stated qualifications, expect screening of your profile and projects, followed by technical conversations on DSA, one backend language, databases and APIs. Treat this as a reasonable expectation, not a confirmed process.

What interviewers will likely test

Since the role stresses programming fundamentals and DSA, revise arrays, strings, hash maps, trees, recursion and complexity analysis. Be ready to explain any project listed on your resume in detail.

How to Apply

  1. Open the official LinkedIn job posting using the link in the Official Links section below.
  2. Sign in to LinkedIn and make sure your profile is complete, with your education, skills and projects.
  3. Update your resume so that it highlights Python, Node.js or Go, plus backend projects, GitHub links and any API or database work.
  4. Click Easy Apply and fill in the form carefully.
  5. Submit the application and keep an eye on LinkedIn and your email for updates.

Apply early

No closing date is disclosed, and LinkedIn postings can be taken down once enough applications arrive. Apply as soon as your resume is ready.

Preparation Plan

Spend the first week on DSA basics and solving easy-to-medium problems in your chosen language. In the second week, build a small REST API with FastAPI, Express.js or a Go framework, connect it to a SQL database such as PostgreSQL, and add tests.

In the third week, learn the basics of NoSQL (for example MongoDB or Redis), Docker and Git workflows. If time permits, read about microservices, message queues such as Kafka, and how LLM-based applications are served. Mention these only if you actually understand them.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Applying without being in the final year, which fails the stated criteria.
  • Listing every language on your resume instead of showing depth in one of Python, Node.js or Go.
  • Having no GitHub links or project proof.
  • Ignoring database fundamentals such as joins, indexing and normalisation.
  • Trusting forwarded messages instead of applying on the official LinkedIn posting. Never pay anyone for this internship.

Official Links and Final Checklist

  • My resume shows backend projects and one primary language.
  • My GitHub and LinkedIn profiles are updated.

Final Takeaway

This is a useful opportunity for final-year engineering students who want hands-on backend experience at an AI healthcare startup. The stipend and last date are not disclosed, so apply through the official link in the Official Links section above and clarify those details if you are shortlisted.
