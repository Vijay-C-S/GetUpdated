POWERGRID Trainee Engineer (Electronics) Recruitment 2026 via GATE 2026 EC: Eligibility, Salary and How to Apply

November 24, 2026 by GetUpdated Team

POWERGRID Trainee Engineer (Electronics) 2026

POWERGRID has opened Trainee Engineer (Electronics) recruitment 2026 through GATE 2026 (EC paper) for PowerTel, its telecom subsidiary. The advertisement is numbered CC/04/2026 (Post ID 569). Applications opened on 13 November 2026, and the last date to apply and pay the fee is 27 November 2026, 23:59 hrs. That leaves only a few days.

This is a good chance for ECE and Electronics graduates who wrote GATE 2026 in the EC paper. There is no written exam beyond GATE. Your GATE score decides the shortlist, and a group discussion and interview decide the rest. No work experience is needed.

Selected candidates are trained for one year in the Rs 30,000-1,20,000 pay scale. After that they become Assistant Engineers (E0) in the same scale. This post covers eligibility, pay, fee, selection, how to apply and how to prepare for the GD and interview.

Apply early

The deadline is 27 November 2026, 23:59 hrs. Portals tend to slow down on the last day, so finish your application today if you can.

About POWERGRID and PowerTel

POWERGRID, the Power Grid Corporation of India, is a central public sector enterprise. It runs the country's main electricity transmission network. Its telecom subsidiary, PowerTel, offers telecom and ICT services built on the grid's fibre infrastructure.

This recruitment is for PowerTel, so the work is in telecom and electronics, not power transmission. For an ECE graduate that matches the degree well. Candidates should read the notification carefully, because a posting at any PowerTel office or establishment in India (or abroad) is possible.

After joining PowerTel, you have no claim to employment in POWERGRID or its other subsidiaries or joint ventures. Keep that in mind if you hoped to join the parent company.

Vacancy Details

The notification lists multiple posts for Trainee Engineer (Electronics). They are spread across categories: UR, OBC (NCL), SC, ST and EWS. Two PwBD posts are counted within these. One is interchangeable, with locomotor disability candidates considered first. The other is reserved for hearing-impaired candidates.

Reservation applies as per Government of India rules. If you claim OBC (NCL) or EWS status, your certificate must be issued on or after 01.04.2026 and based on the financial years the notification specifies. Older certificates can lead to rejection.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Full-time B.E./B.Tech/B.Sc (Engg.) in an electronics-related discipline from a recognised university or institute.
  • At least 60% marks or equivalent CGPA. If your university has no conversion scheme, divide your CGPA by the maximum CGPA and multiply by 100. Marks are not rounded up.
  • You must have appeared in and qualified GATE 2026 in the EC paper with a valid score. Other GATE papers are not accepted.
  • Indian nationals only.
  • Age: 18 years minimum and 28 years maximum as on 27.11.2026.

Salary and Benefits

During the one-year training, trainees draw pay in the Rs 30,000-1,20,000 (IDA) scale, with HRA and perks at 12% of basic pay per month. After training, you become Assistant Engineer (E0) in the same scale. Listings report an approximate CTC of Rs 14.33 lakh per annum.

Promotion from E0 to E1 comes after 5 years, excluding training. Each later level takes 5 years, up to E6. The service bond is Rs 5,00,000 for General, OBC (NCL) and EWS, and Rs 2,50,000 for SC, ST and PwBD. You must serve at least 3 years after training. Think about this before applying, since leaving early means paying the bond.

Application Fee

  • General, OBC (NCL) and EWS: Rs 500, non-refundable.
  • SC, ST and PwBD: exempt.

The notification is inconsistent on exemptions, and a later page also lists "Departmental" candidates as exempt. Check the official website before paying or skipping payment. Applications with a missing fee, where one is due, are rejected.

Selection Process

  1. Shortlisting based on normalised GATE 2026 marks (out of 100), as per criteria set by management.
  2. Behavioural assessment for shortlisted candidates, if required. Its result feeds into the interview.
  3. Group Discussion and Personal Interview, in Hindi or English at your choice.
  4. Document verification and pre-employment medical examination.

Interview qualifying marks are 40% for UR and EWS, and 30% for reserved categories. In a tie, the older candidate ranks higher. GATE is 85% of the final score, so your score decides most of the outcome. The interview still matters because you must clear its qualifying marks.

How to Apply

  1. Open the official POWERGRID careers page through the link in the Official Links section below, and find the Trainee Engineer (Electronics) advertisement.
  2. Register on the portal, even if you applied to POWERGRID before.
  3. Enter your GATE 2026 application number, registration number and name exactly as on your GATE documents. These cannot be changed after submission.
  4. Fill in the form and upload documents. Keep a valid email, an alternate email and a mobile number ready.
  5. Submit the form to get your unique Registration Number.
  6. Log in and pay the fee online, if it applies to you.
  7. Print the submitted application for document verification. Do not send hard copies.

Documents to keep ready

  • Photograph and signature, each 50 KB, in .jpg format.
  • Date of birth proof (Matric certificate or birth certificate).
  • Degree and mark sheets for all years or semesters, combined into one PDF of up to 10 MB.
  • Category or PwBD certificates, if applicable.
  • No-Objection Certificate for government or PSU employees.

Preparation Plan

Your GATE score is already fixed, so the next step is the GD and interview. They carry 15% of the weightage, and you must clear the interview qualifying marks.

  • Revise core ECE: analog and digital electronics, signals and systems, communication systems, networks and electromagnetics. Interviewers often ask about your final-year project and core subjects.
  • Learn telecom basics: optical fibre, transmission media, modulation, networking and basics of data communication. PowerTel works in this field, so such questions are likely.
  • Know the company: read about POWERGRID's role in the grid and what PowerTel does.
  • Practise GD topics: the power sector, renewable energy, digitalisation and technology in public services.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Entering your name or GATE number differently from the GATE scorecard. This cannot be corrected after submission.
  • Using an old OBC (NCL) or EWS certificate that was issued before 01.04.2026.
  • Leaving the form in draft status or skipping the fee when it applies.
  • Waiting for the last day. The form cannot be edited after the deadline.
  • Ignoring the service bond, which can cost you Rs 2.5-5 lakh if you leave early.
  • Giving false information. It can lead to rejection at any stage, even after joining.

You will also need your original photo ID, GATE admit card and score card at the selection stage. Photocopies are not accepted.
