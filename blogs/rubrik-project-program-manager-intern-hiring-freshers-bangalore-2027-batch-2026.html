About Rubrik

Rubrik describes itself as the Security and AI Operations Company. Its platform is built at the intersection of data, identity and AI, and aims to deliver cyber resilience and trusted AI operations for enterprises worldwide. The company is listed on the NYSE under the ticker RBRK.

For a fresher, this means you would be learning inside a product company that sells enterprise data security and recovery software. The posting also lists preferred exposure to storage and enterprise product development, which hints at the kind of products your programs would touch.

Rubrik also states that it is an Equal Opportunity Employer and that it focuses on a merit-based, inclusive culture.

Role Overview: What You Will Actually Do

As the intern, you assist in managing delivery of On-Prem and SaaS products. You help plan, manage and execute several technical programs and initiatives each year across Rubrik's product portfolio. You get first-hand exposure to improving processes across the development, validation and deployment lifecycle.

The official responsibilities fall into four areas:

  • Planning and execution: assist in defining program scope, goals, milestones and success metrics; develop and maintain project plans, schedules and documentation; track progress and update stakeholders; create dashboards or reports for progress and KPIs.
  • Coordination and communication: work with Engineering and Product leadership and with functions such as Product Management and Customer Support to prioritise, plan and deliver features; communicate schedules, status and issues; help run effective meetings.
  • Risk management: identify potential risks, help build mitigation plans and work with development teams to resolve issues that may affect timelines.
  • Continuous improvement: recommend tools and methods that improve efficiency and automation; document lessons learned and best practices.

This is a program-management role, not a coding internship, but it expects technical understanding.

Eligibility Criteria

The posting lists mandatory qualifications and minimum eligibility criteria. Check each one before you apply.

  • Currently pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Information Systems or a related field.
  • CGPA of 8 and above.
  • 2027 graduate from a circuital branch only (the posting's wording; it does not list branches, so ECE, EEE and similar branches are the typical reading, but confirm with the official page).
  • Available from January 2027 to May 2027 in Bangalore for an onsite internship. No remote option.
  • Strong organisational and analytical skills.
  • Basic understanding of the software development lifecycle (SDLC) and agile methods.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication.
  • Ability to learn fast and work collaboratively in a fast-paced team.

Check the branch rule

The posting says "circuital branches only". If your branch is not clearly circuital, do not assume you qualify. Rubrik can reject applications that miss the stated criteria.

Preferred Skills

These are nice to have, not mandatory, but they can set your profile apart:

  • Exposure to cloud platforms such as AWS, Azure or GCP.
  • Exposure to the storage domain.
  • Exposure to enterprise product development.

As a student, you can show this through a college project that used a cloud service, a certification such as AWS Cloud Practitioner, or an internship where you worked on a larger product. Even a documented project plan from a final-year project helps show program-management thinking.

Stipend and Dates

The official posting does not disclose the stipend, the number of openings or an application deadline. We have not stated any figures, because they are not confirmed by Rubrik. The only dates given are the internship window: January 2027 to May 2027.

Apply early

Campus-style internship postings can close without notice once enough applications arrive. Since no last date is published, apply as soon as your resume is ready.

Selection Process

Rubrik's posting does not describe the stages of selection. Typically, companies screen applications against criteria such as CGPA and graduation year, then hold one or more interviews. Treat this as general guidance, not a confirmed Rubrik process.

Because the posting stresses organisation, analysis and communication, expect questions about how you plan work, handle deadlines and explain technical ideas simply. Prepare for a mix of resume-based, behavioural and basic technical questions.

How to Apply

  2. Open the official Rubrik Greenhouse posting through the link in the Official Links section below.
  3. Update your resume to one page, highlighting projects, coordination roles, cloud exposure and your CGPA.

Preparation Plan

Start with the basics the posting names. Revise the SDLC phases (requirements, design, development, testing, deployment, maintenance) and agile ideas such as sprints, backlogs, stand-ups and retrospectives. Learn how a feature moves from idea to release.

Next, build practical program-management skills:

  • Practise making a simple project plan with milestones, owners and risks in a spreadsheet or a free tool such as Trello or Jira's free tier.
  • Build a small dashboard showing progress and a few KPIs. Use it as a talking point in interviews.
  • Learn basic cloud concepts: compute, storage, backup and recovery, and the difference between On-Prem and SaaS.
  • Read about data protection and cyber resilience, since that is Rubrik's field.

Show ownership

Prepare two or three stories where you coordinated people, met a deadline or handled a risk, even in a college project or club event. Use a clear situation, action and result format.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Applying without checking the circuital-branch and CGPA 8 rules.
  • Sending a generic resume that lists only technical skills and no evidence of planning or communication.
  • Assuming the role is remote. It is onsite in Bangalore only.
  • Not being free for the whole January to May 2027 window because of exams or other commitments.
  • Relying on third-party sites for details. Use only the official posting.
