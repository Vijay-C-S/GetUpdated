ECIL Recruitment 2026: Apply Online for 242 Graduate Engineer and Technician Apprentice Posts

November 24, 2026 by GetUpdated Team

ECIL Graduate Engineer & Technician Apprentice 2026

Electronics Corporation of India Limited (ECIL) has opened applications for 242 Apprentice posts, split between 200 Graduate Engineer Apprentices and 42 Technician (Diploma) Apprentices. Online applications started on 24-11-2026 and the last date to apply is 13-Dec-2026.

For engineering and diploma freshers, an apprenticeship at a central public sector electronics company is a strong way to get hands-on industry exposure. There is no application fee, and selection is through document verification and an interview rather than a long written exam.

This guide covers eligibility, stipend, dates, the selection process, how to apply and how to prepare, based on the details published on the job listing. Read the official notification PDF before you apply, because the notification is the final word on every condition.

About ECIL

Electronics Corporation of India Limited is a well-known public sector company in the electronics and technology space, headquartered in Hyderabad. The document verification for this drive is also being held at ECIL's Corporate Learning & Development Centre in Hyderabad.

For a fresher, an apprenticeship here means training inside a large technology organisation. You work with engineers, learn how projects run and build a record that looks good on a resume, especially if you later target PSU or government technical roles.

Eligibility Criteria

Check each point below against the official notification.

  • Graduate Engineer Apprentice: BE/B.Tech degree.
  • Technician Apprentice: Diploma in the relevant discipline.
  • Age limit: maximum 25 years as on 31-12-2026.
  • Experience: no prior work experience is mentioned as a requirement. This is an apprenticeship for freshers.

The listing says training starts on "01st Jan 2026", which cannot be right as the other dates fall in December 2026. It most likely means 1 January 2027. Confirm the joining date in the official notification.

The timeline is very short. Document verification begins just four days after the last date to apply, so your documents should be ready before you submit the form.

Application Fee

There is no application fee for this recruitment. If anyone asks you for money to get an ECIL apprenticeship, treat it as a scam and apply only through official channels.

Selection Process

Selection has two stages:

  1. Document Verification: held at Electronics Corporation of India Limited, Corporate Learning & Development Centre (CLDC), Nalanda Complex, ECIL (PO), Hyderabad - 500 062, from 17th to 19th Dec 2026.
  2. Interview: shortlisted candidates are interviewed. Provisionally selected candidates get offer letters by e-mail on 23rd to 24th Dec 2026.

There is no written exam mentioned, so your academic record, documents and interview performance carry the weight. Carry originals and photocopies of everything to Hyderabad, and plan your travel in advance.

How to Apply

  1. Read the ECIL notification completely and confirm you meet the eligibility, age and qualification conditions.
  2. Keep a working email ID and mobile number ready, since offer letters and updates come by e-mail.
  3. Prepare scanned copies of your ID proof, age proof, educational certificates, mark sheets, resume and a recent photograph.
  4. Open the application link given in the Official Links section below and fill in the online form.
  5. Upload the required documents and photograph in the specified format.
  6. Check every detail and submit the form. No fee payment is needed as per the listing.
  7. Save the application or request number and a copy of the submitted form for future reference.

Preparation Plan

Since the process is document verification plus interview, preparation is mostly about your fundamentals and your confidence.

For Graduate Engineer Apprentices

  • Revise core subjects of your branch: basics of electronics, circuits, digital systems, microcontrollers, communication or whichever applies to your discipline.
  • Be ready to explain your final-year project in simple words: what problem it solved, your role, tools used and what you learned.
  • Revisit lab experiments and be able to explain the principle behind them.

For Technician Apprentices

  • Brush up on diploma-level practicals: soldering, measuring instruments, basic circuit troubleshooting and safety practices.
  • Know your mini-project or workshop experience well.

For everyone

  • Read about ECIL, its products and its role as a public sector company, so you can answer "Why ECIL?".
  • Practise a short, honest self-introduction in English and your comfortable language.

Interview Topics to Expect

Interviews for apprenticeships tend to be fundamentals-driven. Expect questions on your academic background, final-year or mini projects, core subject basics, your interest in electronics and manufacturing, and your willingness to learn on the shop floor or in the lab. You may also be asked about relocation and discipline at work.

Know your own resume inside out. Anything you list is fair game in the interview.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Missing the deadline: 13-Dec-2026 is firm, and verification starts soon after.
  • Ignoring branch conditions: Applying without checking whether your branch is eligible wastes your effort.
  • Age miscalculation: Age is counted as on 31-12-2026, not the date you apply.
  • Incomplete documents: Missing a certificate at verification can cost you the seat.
  • Using wrong email or phone: Offer letters come by e-mail, so a typo can cost you an offer.
  • Trusting unofficial sources blindly: Use the aggregator listing only as a guide and confirm facts in the official PDF.

Final Takeaway

This ECIL drive offers 242 apprenticeship seats with no fee and a simple selection process, which makes it a good option for BE/B.Tech and diploma freshers looking for training at a central PSU. The window is short: apply by 13-Dec-2026 and keep your documents ready for verification from 17th Dec.

Read the official notification fully, confirm the branch and age conditions, and prepare your fundamentals and projects for the interview. Keep checking the ECIL website for any updates after you apply.
