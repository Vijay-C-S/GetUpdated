About Nsemi Design Services India Pvt Ltd

According to its LinkedIn posting, Nsemi Design Services India Pvt Ltd is a technology company specializing in semiconductor innovation and advanced engineering services. LinkedIn lists its industry as Semiconductor Manufacturing.

Semiconductor and chip-design companies hire both technical and non-technical people, and the recruitment team supports both. For an HR fresher, this means exposure to hiring engineers as well as business and support staff.

Role Overview

The Recruitment Intern will support the Talent Acquisition team in managing end-to-end hiring for technical and non-technical roles. The position is full-time and on-site in Bengaluru South, so expect to work from the office rather than from home.

Day-to-day work, as described in the posting, includes:

  • Sourcing candidates through various channels
  • Screening resumes
  • Coordinating interviews between hiring managers and candidates
  • Assisting in initial candidate interactions
  • Maintaining recruitment databases and updating candidate records
  • Supporting onboarding and training coordination
  • Contributing to employer branding initiatives
  • Preparing recruitment-related reports and analytics

This mix gives you a view of the whole recruitment funnel. You learn the process, and you also build the documentation and reporting habits that employers look for in HR professionals.

Preparation Plan for HR Freshers

You do not need to be a semiconductor expert. A few days of focused preparation will help.

Understand the basics of the company

Read the company's LinkedIn page and posts. Learn what semiconductor design and engineering services mean at a basic level, such as chip design, verification and embedded work. Being able to say a few sentences about the industry shows genuine interest.

Brush up recruitment fundamentals

  • The hiring funnel: sourcing, screening, interview, offer, onboarding
  • How to read a job description and pick out must-have and good-to-have skills
  • Boolean search basics for finding candidates on LinkedIn and job portals
  • Basic ATS concepts, such as how candidate records are tracked and updated

Practise your resume screening

Take two or three job descriptions for engineering roles and practise shortlisting sample resumes. Write down why you would accept or reject each one, so you can explain your thinking in an interview.

Get comfortable with MS Office

Practise Excel basics such as filters, pivot tables and simple formulas, because recruitment trackers and reports are often built there. Be ready to discuss how you would present hiring data.

Likely Interview Topics

  • Tell me about yourself and why you chose HR
  • Why recruitment, and why a semiconductor or tech company?
  • How would you find candidates for a hard-to-fill technical role?
  • How do you handle a candidate who does not respond or drops out?
  • How would you schedule interviews between busy managers and candidates?
  • What would you include in a weekly recruitment report?
  • Describe a time you managed several tasks with tight deadlines

Answer with short, real examples from college, projects or earlier internships. Honest answers with clear reasoning work better than memorised definitions.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Sending a generic resume. Mention recruitment-related work, tools and projects near the top.
  • Ignoring the experience line. Address it directly by showing relevant exposure.
  • Skipping company research. Not knowing what the company does is a quick way to lose a recruiter's interest.
  • Poor communication. The job is about communication, so reply to calls and emails professionally and promptly.
  • Unprofessional LinkedIn profile. Recruiters will check yours, so keep it clean and updated.
  • Waiting too long. With no deadline stated, assume the posting can close at any time.
